## 1. Uniformity in the varying primes is genuinely supplied

[IsAlgClosureRat.lean:1844–1903](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/be5528a02f46de803a113ccc224659f32f3acdc0/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/QuadraticExtensions/IsAlgClosureRat.lean#L1844-L1903) proves towerTwo: first obtain g from abRk applied at l=2 to the FIXED field withI M, then set R=4(g+6 deg M), and only then introduce q,d and a representative L of base2 M q d. The rank estimate is not an application of Ferrero–Washington separately to every varying L. The source constructs both the rank bound for XX and IsTower for the relevant ramification parameter 2. [IsAlgClosureRat.lean:1921–1945](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/be5528a02f46de803a113ccc224659f32f3acdc0/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/QuadraticExtensions/IsAlgClosureRat.lean#L1921-L1945) constructs TwoBase with degree <=8 deg M and embedding into the cyclotomic field of conductor 4 M.m q d, and uses this same R in twoIn. The rank bound and the changing conductor are therefore separately controlled. The remaining twoBase compIndex/shaTwo comparison deserves its own audit.

The precise FerreroWashington interface is [FerreroWashington.lean:51–56](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/be5528a02f46de803a113ccc224659f32f3acdc0/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/PublishedTheorems/FerreroWashington.lean#L51-L56): for a fixed abelian K with required roots of unity and prime l, there exists r uniformly bounding #Cl(K')/Cl(K')^l by l^r for every cyclotomic layer K'. It is a bounded mod-l rank consequence, not an assertion of a universal rank for all abelian fields. [FerreroWashington.lean:1500–1515](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/be5528a02f46de803a113ccc224659f32f3acdc0/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/GaloisLibrary/FerreroWashingtonIndex/FerreroWashington.lean#L1500-L1515) supplies an actual theorem body: embed into cyclotomic fields of fixed prime-to-l conductor, use cyc for an eventual bound, transport by desc and degree, and choose D*l^n0*B before n,K'. This is only the inspected top assembly of the project's own Ferrero–Washington provider; cyc, Stickelberger and the transport arithmetic remain outstanding.

## 2. Finite character ranks to a common infinite-layer module

[IsAlgClosureRat.lean:1269–1312](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/be5528a02f46de803a113ccc224659f32f3acdc0/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/QuadraticExtensions/IsAlgClosureRat.lean#L1269-L1312) (infRk) does not take an arbitrary inverse limit of dimension bounds. The character spaces at finite levels restrict to nested images in the characters on the cyclotomic kernel. Every character on that kernel extends to some finite level by xdualExt. The images exhaust the target and each has cardinality <=l^r. A nested union with this finite bound also has cardinality <=l^r. The extension theorem xdualExt is a substantial residual local-condition provider. levelRk (1320ff) supplies the finite-layer estimate used by fwRk; it is to be checked against the actual class field character comparison.

[IsAlgClosureRat.lean:923–990](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/be5528a02f46de803a113ccc224659f32f3acdc0/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/QuadraticExtensions/IsAlgClosureRat.lean#L923-L990) proves genOfRk. A continuous locally constant character from the compact cyclotomic kernel into Q_l/Z_l has finite image; hence it is killed by some l-power. The l-torsion characters inject into the Z/l character module via the inverse of the exact coefficient embedding, preserving both the specified inertia and decomposition annihilation conditions. The dualGen provider converts this l-torsion bound into a generator bound on the R-linear dual. This is not a deduction of finite generation merely from a cardinality bound on arbitrary algebraic duals. The dualGen proof itself remains to inspect. abRk transports the fixed field character bound to every closure of Q using the common field subgroup; no field-representative-dependent g is chosen.

## 3. Why the two extra primes cost a bounded number of characters

Let V be the cyclotomic kernel for withI M in the common absolute Galois group of Q. The baseRk calculation in [IsAlgClosureRat.lean:1873–1892](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/be5528a02f46de803a113ccc224659f32f3acdc0/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/QuadraticExtensions/IsAlgClosureRat.lean#L1873-L1892) first allows ramification at q and d, then passes to the two quadratic extensions. The congruences q=3 mod 8 and d=7 mod 16 are essential.

[IsAlgClosureRat.lean:1519–1553](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/be5528a02f46de803a113ccc224659f32f3acdc0/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/QuadraticExtensions/IsAlgClosureRat.lean#L1519-L1553) (decIndex) produces an arithmetic Frobenius with cyclotomic image p in Z_2^×, using the action on every root of order 2^n; it does not infer equality from a single finite level. The closed image of the decomposition subgroup contains this p. The index bounds 2 and 4 follow from idxAdic for the respective congruences. Classically Z_2^×={±1}×(1+4Z_2); q²-1 has valuation 3 and d²-1 valuation 4, and q,d both have sign -1 modulo 4. Thus the closed cyclic subgroup generated by p has index 2 or 4 respectively, by the 2-adic principal unit logarithm or the lifting-the-exponent identity. A larger closed subgroup has no larger index. The source uses idxAdic rather than this informal argument; its exact proof is residual.

[IsAlgClosureRat.lean:1563–1600](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/be5528a02f46de803a113ccc224659f32f3acdc0/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/QuadraticExtensions/IsAlgClosureRat.lean#L1563-L1600) combines this with the fixed abelian subgroup of index deg M to produce representatives of the V-orbits of valuations above p, with at most 2 deg M for q and 4 deg M for d. The count is of orbits of valuation extensions, not all valuations individually. The source uses supIndex and orbIndex and inertia-conjugation. [IsAlgClosureRat.lean:328–395](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/be5528a02f46de803a113ccc224659f32f3acdc0/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/QuadraticExtensions/IsAlgClosureRat.lean#L328-L395) (charStep) evaluates a character at one chosen tame generator for each such orbit. A difference of two characters with identical evaluations vanishes on all inertia at the new prime, using tameGen and invariance under conjugation inside V. At other primes it already vanishes. Each evaluation has l choices, so loosening ramification multiplies the cardinal bound by at most l^k. This is valid even though the new character space need not split as a direct sum of independent local conditions. The proof checks that the full relevant inertia subgroups lie in V; for the second prime it also uses coprimality of p with the old conductor. tameGen, ineTwo and the finite orbit-count providers are residual, not assumptions to silently drop.

Thus the cardinal bounds after q,d are 2^(g+2deg M) and 2^(g+6deg M).

## 4. Two quadratic steps: characteristic two, not semisimplicity

[QuadStep.lean:397–474](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/be5528a02f46de803a113ccc224659f32f3acdc0/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/QuadraticExtensions/QuadStep.lean#L397-L474) supplies the factor-of-two dimension bound for each quadratic extension, verifying index divides 2, openness, conjugation stability of the forbidden inertia set, and containment in the new subgroup for those inertia elements already lying in the old subgroup. The last condition uses p|N and the conductor of the quadratic field. This is why ramification is loosened first.

For an index-two open U<=V, choose sigma in V\U. On P=CharOff(U,S,2), conjugation gives an involution C, because sigma² lies in U and homomorphisms to an abelian group are invariant under inner conjugation by U. Over F_2, (1+C)²=0, so im(1+C)⊆ker(1+C)=P^C. If #P^C<=n then #P<=n². The code uses exactly this square-zero argument ([QuadStep.lean:289–395](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/be5528a02f46de803a113ccc224659f32f3acdc0/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/QuadraticExtensions/QuadStep.lean#L289-L395)), not a decomposition into + and - eigenspaces, which would be invalid in characteristic 2.

The bound on P^C itself requires care: an invariant character of U need not extend to V, since it may be nonzero on sigma². [QuadStep.lean:203–280](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/be5528a02f46de803a113ccc224659f32f3acdc0/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/QuadraticExtensions/QuadStep.lean#L203-L280) explicitly handles this obstruction. If a character phi0 with phi0(sigma²)=1 exists, choose it once. Given invariant phi, subtract phi0 when necessary so the corrected chi has chi(sigma²)=0. Extend chi to V with value zero on sigma (extTwo). To encode the removed bit, add the quotient sign character epsilon:V/U->F_2, which vanishes on U and on the forbidden inertia elements. Evaluation at sigma recovers that bit, restriction recovers chi, and hence phi. This constructs an injection of P^C into CharOff(V,S,2), not a false extension claim for every invariant character. It is a cardinal comparison, not necessarily a canonical linear map. Therefore the two quadratic steps give R=4(g+6deg M).

No quantifier mismatch found in this upper assembly. In particular the exact rank choice is made before q,d. The lower field-theoretic and class-field character providers remain separate.

## 5. Cyclotomic descent: the fixed module and generator

[ExistsDescends.lean:1073–1196](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/be5528a02f46de803a113ccc224659f32f3acdc0/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/NumberFieldTowers/ExistsDescends.lean#L1073-L1196) constructs the actual isomorphism from Sha of a finite layer to twisted invariants in Dinf on the common cyclotomic kernel. It uses the equality of cyclotomic kernels after embedding the finite field, transports characters preserving local conditions, checks the conjugation and chi^(-e) actions explicitly, and uses the restriction image theorem resInfOnto and injectivity resInfInj. Those two theorems are residual deep descent interfaces; the current inspection does not replace them with an unexplained isomorphism. They require e!=0 and the procyclic condition, both supplied at the final call. In particular a finite layer is not an arbitrary extension.

[ExistsDescends.lean:1313–1359](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/be5528a02f46de803a113ccc224659f32f3acdc0/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/NumberFieldTowers/ExistsDescends.lean#L1313-L1359) proves that the twisted invariants are exactly the kernel of dTw(gamma)-1, equivalently dTw(gamma^-1)-1. Being fixed by the single topological generator of the cyclotomic image is sufficient because each individual character is killed by a power of l and has action factoring through a finite congruence quotient (dinfNil, dinfTors). For any v in the subgroup, approximate its cyclotomic image by an integral power of gamma modulo a sufficiently deep principal unit group, on which that character is fixed. The proof uses continuity of the action on each character in this concrete form, not algebraic generation by gamma of the full absolute Galois group.

[ExistsDescends.lean:1383–1420](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/be5528a02f46de803a113ccc224659f32f3acdc0/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/NumberFieldTowers/ExistsDescends.lean#L1383-L1420) embeds each L_m into a common closure, identifies its subgroup using layerUniq, uses gamma^(l^m) as generator for that layer, and passes from a kernel of the twisted action to a cokernel of its dual via cokerDual. The actual exists_descends chooses gamma before e,m,L_m, obtaining

    shaInv(L_m,l,e)=length coker((xTw(e,gamma))^(l^m)-1).

This matches the field-enlargement inequality in the previous audit. cokerDual and the restriction image theorem, especially local condition preservation at the infinite layer, remain the most important descent subproofs.

## 6. Determinant estimate: a compactness argument produces genuine common centres

[ABoundOfDescends.lean:148–168](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/be5528a02f46de803a113ccc224659f32f3acdc0/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/AdditiveValuationNorms/ABoundOfDescends.lean#L148-L168) proves an ultrametric intersection lemma in Z_l. If balls around integers e have radii r_e with |e-f|<=max(r_e,r_f), any finite collection has a common point (take centre of a smallest ball), then compactness of Z_l gives a point common to all balls.

For u of exact level c and eigenvalue z in a valued algebraic closure, set

    r_e=l^(j+c) |u^(l^j e) z - 1|.

The source [ABoundOfDescends.lean:179–237](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/be5528a02f46de803a113ccc224659f32f3acdc0/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/AdditiveValuationNorms/ABoundOfDescends.lean#L179-L237) verifies the pairwise inequality via the exact norm identity for differences of u^(l^j e) and the ultrametric inequality. If |z|>=1 use |a-b|<=|(a-b)z|; if |z|<1 then |az-1|=1 for units a, so the inequality also holds. It does not assume every eigenvalue is a unit. Therefore choose a single s in Z_l with

    l^(-(j+c)) |s-e| <= |u^(l^j e) z-1|

for ALL e. No claim that z itself admits a logarithm or that the equation u^(l^j e)z=1 has an integral solution is required.

[ABoundOfDescends.lean:246–274](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/be5528a02f46de803a113ccc224659f32f3acdc0/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/AdditiveValuationNorms/ABoundOfDescends.lean#L246-L274) factors the determinant in an algebraic closure and multiplies the inequalities for R eigenvalues, using the isometric field embedding. This produces a valuation bound by the sum of j+c+v_l(s_i-e). [ABoundOfDescends.lean:624–650](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/be5528a02f46de803a113ccc224659f32f3acdc0/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/AdditiveValuationNorms/ABoundOfDescends.lean#L624-L650) lifts the action on a module with R generators to an R-by-R matrix: choose lifts of images of generators once, and the free module surjects onto X, hence its twisted cokernel surjects onto the actual twisted cokernel. The lift is not assumed invertible or diagonalizable. The length-determinant comparison is an independent provider; when determinant vanishes the upper bound is infinity, so there is no claim of finiteness at a centre.

## 7. Growth in layer number, including l=2 and module torsion

[ABoundOfDescends.lean:520–553](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/be5528a02f46de803a113ccc224659f32f3acdc0/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/AdditiveValuationNorms/ABoundOfDescends.lean#L520-L553) bounds the nilpotence exponent of g-1 on X/lX by R, from the dimension <=R. With k=floor(log_l R)+1, l^k>=R, the characteristic-l polynomial identity gives (g^(l^k)-1)X⊆lX. Raising to the next l-power puts the image in l²X. The source uses jZero=l-log R+3, a conservative level beyond this threshold. From that level onward take w=g^(l^n). Then

    w^l-1=(1+w+...+w^(l-1))(w-1).

The geometric sum is congruent to l times identity modulo l²X. Since X is finite generated, Nakayama on the submodule lX (or its quotient by the relevant image) proves lX⊆im(geometric sum); one does NOT divide the endomorphism by l, which would be unsafe for torsion modules. This is phi at [ABoundOfDescends.lean:342–389](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/be5528a02f46de803a113ccc224659f32f3acdc0/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/AdditiveValuationNorms/ABoundOfDescends.lean#L342-L389). The cokernel length of the geometric sum is at most length(X/lX)<=R. Cokernel length of a composite is at most the sum of the individual lengths, proving the one-step growth bound at [ABoundOfDescends.lean:395–413](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/be5528a02f46de803a113ccc224659f32f3acdc0/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/AdditiveValuationNorms/ABoundOfDescends.lean#L395-L413). Iteration proves [ABoundOfDescends.lean:570–608](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/be5528a02f46de803a113ccc224659f32f3acdc0/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/AdditiveValuationNorms/ABoundOfDescends.lean#L570-L608). For earlier m use image containment for divisible powers rather than subtracting a negative layer difference. Twisting by a unit congruent to 1 mod l preserves mod-l unipotence, so the result applies uniformly to all e. This is a valid proof strategy even if X contains l-power torsion.

Combining fixed-level determinant centres and growth yields ABound. This audit verifies the core mathematical architecture and explicit operations, not a fresh Lean certificate for every provider.
