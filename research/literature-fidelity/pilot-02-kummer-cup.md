## 2. Kummer/Bockstein compatibility, with the actual sign

Let U be the S-units in F_S, D=mu_n, and let delta_K:H_c^1(U)->H_c^2(D) be the compact Kummer boundary and beta:H^1(Z/n)->H²(Z) the ordinary Bockstein. The coefficient inclusion i:D->U sends the compact cup of delta_K(a) and z to

    i_*(delta_K(a) cup z) = a cup beta(z) in H_c^3(U).

[BaseCupKummer.lean:338–360](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/982c2e6a11575837d04f119c56c3b75b3c1c6281/src/GroundTheory/ProfiniteCohomology/RestrictedRamification/BaseCupKummer.lean#L338-L360) specializes the signed connecting-map product identity compactCup_compactδ_add_compactCup_δ in degree 1: the sum of the two terms with sign (-1)^1 is zero. The source explicitly identifies the coefficient maps on both the global and local short exact sequences, rather than conflating the compact Kummer boundary with the ordinary Bockstein. The underlying general connecting-map product lemma still merits its own audit.

[IsDiscreteRepResIntRepTrivGs.lean:76–143](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/982c2e6a11575837d04f119c56c3b75b3c1c6281/src/GroundTheory/ProfiniteCohomology/RestrictedRamification/IsDiscreteRepResIntRepTrivGs.lean#L76-L143) checks at the cocycle level that the cup of a local invariant unit a with beta(z) equals galCupClass for the restricted Q/Z-valued character. With integer representatives b(g) in [0,n-1], the cocycle exponent is B(g,h)=(b(g)+b(h)-b(gh))/n; the character class and Bockstein have exactly the same exponent. [ClassFieldGSLocal.lean:129–175](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/982c2e6a11575837d04f119c56c3b75b3c1c6281/src/GroundTheory/ProfiniteCohomology/RestrictedRamification/ClassFieldGSLocal.lean#L129-L175) evaluates its local invariant by the corresponding local Artin element and the global S-idele character. This uses the unit-first cup with an integral degree-two character class, not the swapped degree-one cup of the normalization pilot.

## 3. Why a non-invariant global element can enter the pairing

A datum d=(t,(a_v)) consists of an S-invariant class of a multiplicative element t (g(t)/t is an S-unit) and local multiplicative elements a_v. It defines a compact class a with global cocycle d(t) and local coordinates given by t's images times a_v. Consider first the datum (t,1). In additive cochain notation write m=t in the larger multiplicative representation X and x=d m in U. If e is the degree-two Bockstein cocycle, x cup e is n-torsion in H³(U), and its class vanishes by the primary H³ theorem reviewed last round (n=l^k). Choose a degree-two U-cochain q with d q=x cup e. In X set W=m cup e - i(q). By Leibniz dW=0. Multiplicatively W(g,h) differs from t^B(g,h) by an S-unit. The compact cup class is the local-to-compact image of res(W): subtract the compact coboundary with global part q and zero local component from the original cup cocycle. Its global part becomes zero and the local component is res(m cup e)-res(q).

[PoitouTateNineTermGs.lean:615–657,761–819](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/982c2e6a11575837d04f119c56c3b75b3c1c6281/src/GroundTheory/ProfiniteCohomology/PoitouTate/PoitouTateNineTermGs.lean#L761-L819) constructs W with exactly this property via exists_compactCup_const and the cone pairing boundary lemma. [886–928](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/982c2e6a11575837d04f119c56c3b75b3c1c6281/src/GroundTheory/ProfiniteCohomology/PoitouTate/PoitouTateNineTermGs.lean#L886-L928) splits d into its global-element and local-unit contributions, combines the Kummer/Bockstein identity with the cone boundary product, and obtains the local image of res(W)+a_v cup beta(z). This is a cohomological equality after the coefficient maps, not a claim that the original mu_n cocycle equals the multiplicative cocycle.

## 4. Outside-S divisor contribution and the archimedean correction

[244–312](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/982c2e6a11575837d04f119c56c3b75b3c1c6281/src/GroundTheory/ProfiniteCohomology/PoitouTate/PoitouTateNineTermGs.lean#L244-L312) computes the invariant outside S. For v outside S, S-units have zero valuation. Consequently ord_v(W)=ord_v(t) B as a two-cochain. Choose a base-field element pi_v with valuation 1 at v (no requirement that it have zero valuation at every other prime). The cocycle pi_v^(ord_v(t) B) has equal valuation image. The source uses gsLocalRes_eq_zero_iff to identify the local Brauer classes of their difference; this is the unramified valuation comparison, not an assertion that arbitrary Brauer classes with equal pointwise valuations are equal. Local reciprocity then evaluates the invariant as the S-idele character of the single outside-S divisor ord_v(t)[v]. That valuation comparison provider is a residual independent check.

[339–476](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/982c2e6a11575837d04f119c56c3b75b3c1c6281/src/GroundTheory/ProfiniteCohomology/PoitouTate/PoitouTateNineTermGs.lean#L339-L476) forms the actual global Brauer class B=A-C, where A is the inflation of W and C is the S-unit Brauer image of a global correction supplied by cone exactness. Its invariants on S equal those of a prescribed tuple beta', and outside S equal the divisor character. Reciprocity gives

    s1 + divChar(div(t)) + s_infty = 0,

where s1=sum_{v in S} inv_v(beta'_v). The source carefully does NOT assume B itself is n-torsion. It proves the infinite-place SUM is killed by 2; when n is odd it is also killed by n because the other two terms are n-torsion. Bezout makes that sum vanish. If the field is totally imaginary each infinite component vanishes separately. Thus s1=-divChar(div(t)). The proof avoids the tempting unjustified assertion that every infinite component of the auxiliary class is n-torsion.

## 5. Actual equality with the S-idele character

[949–976](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/982c2e6a11575837d04f119c56c3b75b3c1c6281/src/GroundTheory/ProfiniteCohomology/PoitouTate/PoitouTateNineTermGs.lean#L949-L976) checks the datum class in the idelic presentation: local components a_v and the NEGATIVE outside-S divisor -div(t). Applying the character yields the sum of local character values minus divChar(div(t)).

[1089–1138](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/982c2e6a11575837d04f119c56c3b75b3c1c6281/src/GroundTheory/ProfiniteCohomology/PoitouTate/PoitouTateNineTermGs.lean#L1089-L1138) proves the compact trace cup identity. Choose a local H²(mu_n) tuple mapping to the compact cup, possible by global H³(mu_n)=0. After the inclusion in units, subtract the W term and local-unit cup terms from Section 3. Cone exactness shows that difference is the restriction of a global S-unit H² class. Apply the computation of Section 4 to the tuple beta' formed by subtracting just the local-unit cups from the original local tuple. Its invariant SUM is n-torsion because both summands are n-torsion by their scaled invariant formulas. Adding back the local cup values now gives exactly

    zmodToQZ(T_F(delta_K(d) cup z)) = zmodToQZ(chi(class(d))).

Here the notation delta_K(d) includes the datum-to-compact-class and the roots-coefficient comparison isomorphism. Injectivity of zmodToQZ yields the Z/n equality (1236ff). The explicit signs agree: the positive local contributions and the negative divisor contribution coincide with the definition of the idele class; no arbitrary unit scaling occurs in this fixed-field base pairing. This identity is consumed by both base nondegeneracy proofs in the same file (1255ff and 1303ff).
