## 1. From the S-unit Brauer comparison to H^3(G_S,mu_l)=0

Assume l is prime, all finite primes above l lie in S, and either l is odd or the number field F is totally imaginary. Put U=O_(F_S,S)^×, L=F_S^×. The short exact Kummer sequence 0 -> mu_l -> U --l--> U -> 0 is provided by sUnitsKummerSequence_shortExact. Its exact sequence contains

    H^2(G_S,U) --l--> H^2(G_S,U) -> H^3(G_S,mu_l)
      -> H^3(G_S,U) --l--> H^3(G_S,U).

[Dimension.lean:90–129](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/57871484bf71ea61b5d98b6505260ed79d25cc88/src/GroundTheory/ProfiniteCohomology/RestrictedRamification/Dimension.lean#L90-L129) proves the needed abstract implication: H^3(G_S,U) has no l-torsion and the l-primary part of H^2(G_S,U) is l-divisible. An element of H^3(mu_l) is killed by l, so its image in H^3(U) vanishes. A preimage under the connecting map may be replaced by its l-primary component because positive-degree cohomology of a discrete module over a profinite group is torsion. Divisibility then makes the connecting class zero. This avoids claiming divisibility of all of H^2(U).

[ExistsNsmulEqLocalPrimaryKernel.lean:98–160](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/57871484bf71ea61b5d98b6505260ed79d25cc88/src/GroundTheory/ProfiniteCohomology/RestrictedRamification/ExistsNsmulEqLocalPrimaryKernel.lean#L98-L160) proves the required divisibility via the previously reviewed S-unit Brauer comparison with the l-primary local invariant sum-zero kernel. Choose a componentwise l-th preimage s0 of a finite-support tuple t (using Q/Z at finite places and zero at infinite places). The chosen preimages may not have zero sum. Set c=sum inv(s0), so l*c=0. Choose a finite place v0 above l, which belongs to S, and subtract the tuple supported at v0 with invariant c. The corrected s has l*s=t, zero sum, and support still contained in the allowed set. The code checks l-primary torsion (one larger exponent). This correction is crucial: componentwise divisibility alone does not prove divisibility of a kernel. For l=2, real places would introduce a nondivisible Z/2 component, so the stated hypothesis cannot simply be dropped. This does not prove that the sum-zero kernel is divisible in cases with no suitable finite place.

## 2. The actual H^3(S-units) primary-vanishing proof

[SurjectiveOfInjectiveOfInjective.lean:326–448](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/57871484bf71ea61b5d98b6505260ed79d25cc88/src/GroundTheory/ProfiniteCohomology/RestrictedRamification/SurjectiveOfInjectiveOfInjective.lean#L326-L448) proves H^3(G_S,U)[l^infty]=0. For a primary class z:
- Transport the quotient G_F/ker(G_F -> G_S) and the fixed-field multiplicative coefficients into the project's units representation. Its image in H^3(G_S,L) inflates to zero in H^3(G_F,Fbar^×). The proof invokes the degree-three inflation injectivity on l-primary classes using H^1 of the kernel equal to zero (Hilbert 90) and prime-to-l annihilation of H^2 of the kernel. It does not assert unrestricted injectivity in degree three. Lines 329–410.
- By exactness z=delta(p) for p in H^2(G_S,L/U). Replace p by a primary preimage p'. Lift its divisor cohomology image to x in H^2(G_S,L) and replace x by a primary preimage x'. The difference p'-image(x') is primary and lies in the kernel of H^2(L/U) -> H^2(D). The divisor quotient C is elementwise annihilated by integers prime to l (previous audit); this also holds for H^1(C). Hence the difference is zero. The connecting map annihilates the image of H^2(L), giving z=0. Lines 411–448.

The divisor lifting at lines 227–297 is not assumed: outside-S valuation evaluations factor through unramified characters, which are detected by a Frobenius lift. Finite l^n-torsion of Q/Z allows a counting argument to realize the desired evaluation using a global class with no other outside-S local components. Sum over the finite support of the divisor cohomology class. The FrobData hypotheses are explicitly supplied at lines 456–466 using the unramified degree-one lift. The detailed gvChar comparison, exists_gsLocalRes_eq_single, degree-three inflation criterion, H^3(G_F,Fbar^×) vanishing and kernel Brauer annihilation are important remaining provider checks. The argument is an identifiable arithmetic proof, not a proof of H^3(U)=0 for arbitrary coefficients or all primes.

## 3. The cohomological dimension criterion really quantifies over open subgroups

[PrimaryComponentCohomologyEqBotOfScdpLt.lean:55–71](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/57871484bf71ea61b5d98b6505260ed79d25cc88/src/GroundTheory/ProfiniteCohomology/CohomologicalDimension/PrimaryComponentCohomologyEqBotOfScdpLt.lean#L55-L71) defines cdp by vanishing of l-primary cohomology of every discrete torsion coefficient in degrees above the bound. The infimum is justified by cdp_le_iff. This is the conventional p-cohomological dimension rather than strict cohomological dimension (which allows all discrete modules).

[Dimension.lean:215–244](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/57871484bf71ea61b5d98b6505260ed79d25cc88/src/GroundTheory/ProfiniteCohomology/RestrictedRamification/Dimension.lean#L215-L244) supplies the correct quantifier: for *every* open subgroup V of G_S, H^3(V,mu_l)=0. The finite extension F'/F corresponding to V lies in F_S; the open subgroup/base-change theorem transports the roots-of-unity coefficient. The places above l lie in the lifted S, and total imaginarity persists under extension.

[DimensionSylow.lean:77–144](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/57871484bf71ea61b5d98b6505260ed79d25cc88/src/GroundTheory/ProfiniteCohomology/RestrictedRamification/DimensionSylow.lean#L77-L144) chooses a pro-l Sylow subgroup P, uses equality of cdp with that of P, obtains H^{n+1}(P,A)=0 by the closed-subgroup restriction/colimit theorem from the vanishing on open overgroups, and proves that P acts trivially on A with underlying additive group Z/l. Mathematically the last fact is because the automorphism group has order l-1; the source uses the open stabilizer and l-power iteration. Transport to the trivial Z/l module then applies the pro-l dimension criterion. The proof does not substitute G_S(l), the maximal pro-l quotient, for P. This establishes cdp <=2 at the source level under the stated arithmetic inputs. The closed-subgroup colimit theorem and Sylow dimension theorem are separate residual dependencies.

[Dimension.lean:155–166,256–264](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/57871484bf71ea61b5d98b6505260ed79d25cc88/src/GroundTheory/ProfiniteCohomology/RestrictedRamification/Dimension.lean#L155-L166) passes to n-torsion coefficients with n odd or F totally imaginary by checking every prime dividing n; each primary component vanishes in degrees >=3 and n annihilates cohomology. n=1 is covered because the coefficient is zero. No finiteness of the coefficient is needed for this vanishing statement.

## 4. Finiteness is separate from dimension

[FiniteRoots.lean:70–117](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/57871484bf71ea61b5d98b6505260ed79d25cc88/src/GroundTheory/ProfiniteCohomology/RestrictedRamification/FiniteRoots.lean#L70-L117) proves H^1(mu_n) finite using the Kummer S-unit map and the finite S-class group; its kernel contribution is a quotient of a finitely generated S-unit group killed by n. For H^2(mu_n), the Kummer sequence bounds the kernel by H^1(U), which injects into the S-class group, and the image by H^2(U)[n]. Lines 137–203 show H^2(U)[n] injects into the finite product of (Q/Z)[n] over finite places in S and the infinite places. Outside-S local invariants vanish. Here the source uses primary Brauer injectivity prime by prime, rather than assuming a general finite cohomology result. Unlike the higher-degree vanishing, these low-degree finiteness proofs do not require excluding real places at l=2.

[Finite.lean:177–274](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/57871484bf71ea61b5d98b6505260ed79d25cc88/src/GroundTheory/ProfiniteCohomology/RestrictedRamification/Finite.lean#L177-L274) proves finiteness for arbitrary finite modules in degrees <=2: choose an open subgroup fixing the roots of unity of order #M, transport trivial cyclic modules to roots-of-unity modules, extend to trivial finite modules and then use coinduction. In [LocalClassFormation/FiniteVanishing/Devissage.lean:166–222](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/57871484bf71ea61b5d98b6505260ed79d25cc88/src/GroundTheory/ProfiniteCohomology/LocalClassFormation/FiniteVanishing/Devissage.lean#L166-L222) the short exact sequence 0 -> M -> Coind_V^U Res(M) -> Q -> 0 uses an open V fixing M inside the chosen roots-of-unity subgroup. Shapiro gives finiteness of H^{r+1} of the middle term and degree induction gives finiteness of H^r(Q). Exactness implies finiteness of H^{r+1}(M). The induction is on degree, not the order of Q. The cyclic-to-trivial provider and Shapiro proof are not independently elaborated here.

[PoitouTate/ZmodToQZCompactCohomologyResMapOfLocalInv.lean:725–748](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/57871484bf71ea61b5d98b6505260ed79d25cc88/src/GroundTheory/ProfiniteCohomology/PoitouTate/ZmodToQZCompactCohomologyResMapOfLocalInv.lean#L725-L748) combines low-degree finiteness with higher-degree vanishing. It checks that primes dividing #M also divide n if M is finite and killed by n; cardinality itself need not divide n.

## 5. Compact support is an actual shifted mapping cone

[CompactSupportCohomology/HomApplyHomApplyEqOfCompEq.lean:113–163,226–237](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/57871484bf71ea61b5d98b6505260ed79d25cc88/src/GroundTheory/ProfiniteCohomology/CompactSupportCohomology/HomApplyHomApplyEqOfCompEq.lean#L113-L163) constructs the localization cone, in degree r:

    C_c^r = C^r(G_S,M) x product_v C^{r-1}(G_v,M)
    d(x,y) = (d_G x, res(x) - d_loc y),

with the second factor zero in degree 0. The code proves d^2=0 via commutation of restriction with the differential. The convention is isomorphic to Cone(res)[-1] (depending on cone sign convention). The projection and inclusion form a degreewise split short exact sequence of complexes; the actual long exact sequence is supplied by the cone homology construction and transported to local cohomology products at [CompactSupportCohomology.lean:127–194](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/57871484bf71ea61b5d98b6505260ed79d25cc88/src/GroundTheory/ProfiniteCohomology/CompactSupportCohomology.lean#L127-L194).

In [Dimension.lean:273–293](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/57871484bf71ea61b5d98b6505260ed79d25cc88/src/GroundTheory/ProfiniteCohomology/RestrictedRamification/Dimension.lean#L273-L293), the exact segment

    product_(v in S) H^3(G_v,M) -> H_c^4(G_S,M) -> H^4(G_S,M)

has both outer terms zero: local absolute Galois groups have l-cd <=2 and so does G_S under the global hypotheses. Thus H_c^4=0, with no use of Poitou–Tate duality in this deduction. The compact cohomology finiteness proof [ZmodToQZCompactCohomologyResMapOfLocalInv.lean:604–635](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/57871484bf71ea61b5d98b6505260ed79d25cc88/src/GroundTheory/ProfiniteCohomology/PoitouTate/ZmodToQZCompactCohomologyResMapOfLocalInv.lean#L604-L635) similarly uses the finite local and global cohomology groups on either side (and the degree-zero injection). Finite S is necessary here for the product finiteness argument.

The finite-place-only cone is compatible with the previously identified odd/totally imaginary regime, where the conventional modified Tate cohomology terms at archimedean places vanish. It must not be confused with an unmodified cone including ordinary H^0 at all infinite places.
