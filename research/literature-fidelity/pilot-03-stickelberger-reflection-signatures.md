## 1. Stickelberger: derive integral annihilators without division in a class group

Write K=Q(mu_M), M>0, G=(Z/M)^×, and sigma_t(zeta)=zeta^t. For a,b in Z/M put c(a,b)=1 if their least nonnegative representatives add to at least M, and 0 otherwise. The actual target of carryAll is

    prod_(t in G) sigma_t^(-1)(C) ^ c(at,bt) = 1

for every ideal class C. This inverse action is important: conjI is a COMAP of a prime ideal under sigma_t, equal to its image under sigma_t^(-1), as specified in StickelbergerAt.lean:82–102.

### 1.1 Degree-one primes and the finite-field calculation

[StickelbergerTheorem/DegOne.lean:50–63](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/060c74468ebd536b7d5193975146e2b46c78f250/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/GaloisLibrary/StickelbergerTheorem/DegOne.lean#L50-L63) chooses a prime P in the specified class outside any finite set using degree-one Chebotarev for the Hilbert class field and the actual Artin equivalence. The residue field has prime order p. Excluding divisors of M ensures gcd(p,M)=1. In a cyclotomic field the existence of such a degree-one prime implies M | p-1; redRoot supplies this together with preservation of the primitive root modulo P. The degree-one Chebotarev, Hilbert class field Artin equivalence and reduction/completion providers still need independent checks; the proof does not claim that a general prime ideal is degree one or principal.

[StickelbergerTheorem/ImportsAlt.lean:84–145](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/060c74468ebd536b7d5193975146e2b46c78f250/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/GaloisLibrary/StickelbergerTheorem/ImportsAlt.lean#L84-L145) constructs a multiplicative character chi on F_p of order M with values in O_K, via teich. For d=(p-1)/M and nonzero a,b,a+b in Z/M, take the Jacobi sum J(a,b)=sum_x chi(x)^a chi(1-x)^b, interpreting the character powers with the actual MulChar convention at zero. The nontriviality checks are explicit. Reduction of sigma_t(J) modulo P gives

    sum_(x in F_p) x^i (1-x)^j,
    i=d val(at), j=d val(bt), 0<i,j<p-1.

[StickelbergerTheorem/FfsumPow.lean:94–143](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/060c74468ebd536b7d5193975146e2b46c78f250/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/GaloisLibrary/StickelbergerTheorem/FfsumPow.lean#L94-L143) expands (1-x)^j. Since 0<i+m<2(p-1), the power sum is zero unless i+m=p-1, in which case it is -1. The latter contribution is nonzero: j<p, so p cannot divide binomial(j,p-1-i) for an admissible index. Thus the reduced Jacobi sum vanishes exactly when i+j<p-1. In particular membership in sigma_t^(-1) P corresponds to carry=0, not carry=1. The complementary set is used below.

[StickelbergerTheorem/JacDvd.lean:51–69](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/060c74468ebd536b7d5193975146e2b46c78f250/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/GaloisLibrary/StickelbergerTheorem/JacDvd.lean#L51-L69) proves J divides p inside O_K by applying the Jacobi-sum inverse-product identity in its fraction field, J(chi,psi)J(chi^-1,psi^-1)=p, then using injectivity of the fraction-field embedding to descend the equality. All three characters must be nontrivial; the caller supplies this. The inverse character Jacobi sum is integral, so this is genuine divisibility of elements, not just a norm equality.

### 1.2 Carry relation and floor relation

[StickelbergerTheorem/ImportsAlt.lean:157–235](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/060c74468ebd536b7d5193975146e2b46c78f250/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/GaloisLibrary/StickelbergerTheorem/ImportsAlt.lean#L157-L235) uses splitInj and splitProd to identify (p) as the product of distinct sigma_t^(-1)P. Because J divides p, the ideal (J) is the product of exactly the primes with carry=0 (sqfree). Their class product is 1. The product of ALL the primes is also 1 because (p) is principal. Therefore the complementary product, with carry=1, is 1. The cases a=0, b=0, and a+b=0 are treated separately, by empty or full products; no Jacobi formula is applied to a trivial character. [StickelbergerTheorem/ImportsAlt.lean:238–263](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/060c74468ebd536b7d5193975146e2b46c78f250/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/GaloisLibrary/StickelbergerTheorem/ImportsAlt.lean#L238-L263) uses the degree-one prime representative described above to obtain the result for any C.

The elementary identity

    floor((k+1) val(t)/M)
      = floor(k val(t)/M)+c(kt,t)

then yields, by induction, prod_t x_t^floor(k val(t)/M)=1 for x_t=sigma_t^-1(C), every k>=0 ([StickelbergerTheorem/ImportsAlt.lean:288–301](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/060c74468ebd536b7d5193975146e2b46c78f250/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/GaloisLibrary/StickelbergerTheorem/ImportsAlt.lean#L288-L301)).

Finally suppose beta,gamma are integer coefficient functions with

    M gamma(t)=sum_s beta(s) val(st).

Let F(t)=sum_s beta(s) floor(val(s)val(t)/M), B=sum_s beta(s) val(s). The division-with-remainder formula implies M(gamma(t)+F(t))=B val(t). Evaluate at t=1 (M>1), giving M|B. For M=1 all residue representatives vanish, treated separately. Write B=M b, cancel M IN Z, and obtain gamma(t)=b val(t)-F(t). Each of these summands is a known integral floor annihilator (use k=M for val(t)). Therefore prod_t x_t^gamma(t)=1. [StickelbergerTheorem/ImportsAlt.lean:310–403](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/060c74468ebd536b7d5193975146e2b46c78f250/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/GaloisLibrary/StickelbergerTheorem/ImportsAlt.lean#L310-L403) is the actual proof of Stick.coef. There is no cancellation of multiplication by M on the class group, which would be invalid if M divides the class number. This supplies the integral Stickelberger relation needed by stickH in the preceding note, without using the full rational group ring action on torsion.

Residual: splitInj/splitProd, sqfree, redRoot/teich, the underlying degree-one Chebotarev and Artin providers, and exact classical literature attribution. The inspected carry, floor and integral annihilation deductions have no identified discrepancy.

## 2. Odd reflection

[FerreroWashingtonIndex/ReflOdd.lean:762–848](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/060c74468ebd536b7d5193975146e2b46c78f250/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/GaloisLibrary/FerreroWashingtonIndex/ReflOdd.lean#L762-L848) specializes the general reflect statement to F=K, n=l an odd prime, trivial equivariance characters, and complex conjugation of the closure. With C=Cl(K), J its conjugation action and additive notation, it proves

    #C/(l C+(1-J)C) <= l * #(ker(1+J) intersect C[l]).

The left side counts the characters of an elementary abelian l-group, identified with the positive conjugation eigenspace of unramified mod-l characters via pullEq. The proof constructs the injection from characters of this quotient into ClEig explicitly. The condition N=1 means unramified at every finite prime, not just outside l.

[FerreroWashingtonIndex/ReflOdd.lean:550–590](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/060c74468ebd536b7d5193975146e2b46c78f250/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/GaloisLibrary/FerreroWashingtonIndex/ReflOdd.lean#L550-L590) checks the actual Kummer twist. If a homomorphism phi corresponds to beta with u(beta)/beta=zeta^phi(u), beta^n=a, then equivariance by psi and cyclotomic action by chi give the ideal class twist chi psi^-1. In particular a conjugation-invariant homomorphism has a radical class satisfying bar(a)=a^-1 mod n-th powers because conjugation sends zeta to zeta^-1. This yields radCl(a) in the minus class group and in C[n]. The sign is not a choice of eigenspace; it follows from action on the coefficient root. The Kummer radical existence and valuation divisibility provider radXDual, and radClMap/radClKer, remain to inspect.

[FerreroWashingtonIndex/ReflOdd.lean:684–735](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/060c74468ebd536b7d5193975146e2b46c78f250/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/GaloisLibrary/FerreroWashingtonIndex/ReflOdd.lean#L684-L735) constructs the map from the character space to the class group by reflMap, with range in the indicated twisted minus torsion subgroup. In its kernel, radCl(a)=1 implies a=u b^n with u a global unit. The condition a bar(a) in (K^×)^n then implies u bar(u) in (K^×)^n. In a CM field u/bar(u) is torsion (the actual proof invokes unitsMulComplexConjInv). Thus u² is a root of unity times an n-th power. For odd n, gcd(2,n)=1, so u itself belongs to that subgroup. [FerreroWashingtonIndex/ReflOdd.lean:602–643](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/060c74468ebd536b7d5193975146e2b46c78f250/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/GaloisLibrary/FerreroWashingtonIndex/ReflOdd.lean#L602-L643) and [FerreroWashingtonIndex/ReflOdd.lean:669–682](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/060c74468ebd536b7d5193975146e2b46c78f250/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/GaloisLibrary/FerreroWashingtonIndex/ReflOdd.lean#L669-L682) implement this step. Hence after changing beta by a base-field factor, beta is itself torsion, while its Kummer character is unchanged. This uses oddness essentially, and is not applied at 2.

[FerreroWashingtonIndex/ReflOdd.lean:306–353](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/060c74468ebd536b7d5193975146e2b46c78f250/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/GaloisLibrary/FerreroWashingtonIndex/ReflOdd.lean#L306-L353) proves that the torsion-root characters inject into the coefficient invariants: choose a common cyclic group of roots of unity containing all the relevant beta (exists_gen, whose proof remains to check), choose u0 generating the image of its Kummer homomorphism, and evaluate phi at u0. A torsion beta is a power of the common root, so this value determines the whole character. Conjugation on torsion roots commutes, which forces the evaluation to lie in the stated coefficient invariants. For the specialization the invariants have at most l elements. Thus #ker<=l and the range bound gives the asserted reflection inequality. No assumption that all Kummer radicals are units or all invariant classes lift is made.

The finite counting consequence in FerreroWashington.lean:1235ff was already audited: the minus l-torsion lies in im(1-J) for odd l, giving the total rank bound. Remaining deep providers: Kummer realization, radCl kernel and equivariance, class field pullEq, torsion-root generator and extension proof. The reflection deduction itself is now source-compared.

## 3. Dyadic replacement for reflection: signatures and the Hasse norm theorem

For K=Q(mu_M), M=f*2^n with f odd >1, n>=2, set C=Cl(K), D=im(1-J), E=ker(1+J). D subset E. The needed bound is on [E:D], NOT on a splitting into plus and minus eigenspaces over F_2. The source proves it using a signature quotient of totally real elements.

### 3.1 The two special arithmetic hypotheses are actually supplied

[FerreroWashingtonIndex/SgnCyc.lean:1088–1175](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/060c74468ebd536b7d5193975146e2b46c78f250/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/GaloisLibrary/FerreroWashingtonIndex/SgnCyc.lean#L1088-L1175) proves that u->u/bar(u) from O_K^× onto the roots of unity is surjective. M is divisible by 4 and is not a prime power, since f>1 is odd. Therefore 1-zeta is a unit (cyclotomic polynomial value at 1). Its quotient by its conjugate is -zeta, which is a primitive M-th root because M=4k and gcd(2k+1,4k)=1. The torsion group has order M. The unit thus maps to its generator. This does not hold by the same proof for pure 2-power conductor; the hypotheses explicitly exclude that case.

[FerreroWashingtonIndex/SgnCyc.lean:1263–1286](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/060c74468ebd536b7d5193975146e2b46c78f250/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/GaloisLibrary/FerreroWashingtonIndex/SgnCyc.lean#L1263-L1286) proves the quadratic extension K/K^+ is unramified at every finite prime. Any nontrivial relative inertia must be complex conjugation. In a cyclotomic field, inertia at p fixes the prime-to-p conductor roots, so conjugation in inertia would force the prime-to-p part of M to divide 2. This is impossible: at p=2 that part is f>1 odd, and for odd p it is divisible by 4. The proof calls cycIne and checks the congruence in unrJ_arith. The exact local cyclotomic inertia theorem remains a separate provider.

### 3.2 Map the class quotient into signature patterns

[FerreroWashingtonIndex/SgnCyc.lean:472–544](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/060c74468ebd536b7d5193975146e2b46c78f250/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/GaloisLibrary/FerreroWashingtonIndex/SgnCyc.lean#L472-L544): for x in E, choose an integral ideal I with class x. Then I bar(I)=(b) is principal and stable under conjugation. The quotient bar(b)/b is a unit of absolute value 1 at all infinite places, hence torsion. The preceding unit surjectivity permits changing the generator by a unit to obtain a nonzero a in K^+ with I bar(I)=(a). In particular this does not assert that every conjugation-stable principal ideal has a real generator without the unit hypothesis.

If a is totally positive and I bar(I)=(a), then a is a local norm at every place of K^+. At infinity this is positivity for the complex-to-real norm. At finite places [FerreroWashingtonIndex/SgnCyc.lean:240–296](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/060c74468ebd536b7d5193975146e2b46c78f250/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/GaloisLibrary/FerreroWashingtonIndex/SgnCyc.lean#L240-L296) chooses b and a locally invertible t such that b bar(b)=a t via ideal_step. The element N(b)/a is then a unit at the place. Since the quadratic extension is unramified at finite places, the local norm on units is surjective (the imported integerUnitsOver norm theorem). Thus a is a local norm, even if it was not initially a unit. [FerreroWashingtonIndex/SgnCyc.lean:419–458](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/060c74468ebd536b7d5193975146e2b46c78f250/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/GaloisLibrary/FerreroWashingtonIndex/SgnCyc.lean#L419-L458) applies the quadratic Hasse norm theorem to write a=beta bar(beta). Then the fractional ideal I/(beta) has product with its conjugate equal to 1. By prime factorization, an anti-invariant divisor lies in im(1-J): its coefficients vanish at fixed primes and are opposite on two-element orbits. The source implements this after clearing denominators, using classGroup_div_mem_of_mul_conj_eq at [FerreroWashingtonIndex/SgnCyc.lean:149–189](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/060c74468ebd536b7d5193975146e2b46c78f250/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/GaloisLibrary/FerreroWashingtonIndex/SgnCyc.lean#L149-L189). Hence x lies in D.

Let V be the additive F_2-space of even sign functions on embeddings (signs agree on conjugate pairs), and S the signatures of real units, defined via additive subgroup closure. Every element of S is represented by a real unit since signs are multiplicative in additive notation; hMinus_unit verifies this. Choose one ideal and real generator for each representative in E/D. If two generator signs differ by an element of S, multiply their product by the corresponding real unit to make it totally positive (sign addition is subtraction in F_2). Apply the preceding Hasse argument to bar(I)J' to conclude the classes differ by D. [FerreroWashingtonIndex/SgnCyc.lean:645–714](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/060c74468ebd536b7d5193975146e2b46c78f250/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/GaloisLibrary/FerreroWashingtonIndex/SgnCyc.lean#L645-L714) constructs a SET injection E/D -> V/S using chosen quotient representatives. It need not prove that the chosen signs define a canonical group homomorphism. This suffices for [E:D]<=[V:S]. The local ideal approximation, local norm theorem, Hasse norm theorem and prime-factorization lemma are substantial independent providers.

### 3.3 Bound the signature quotient by the same uniform nilpotent exponent

[FerreroWashingtonIndex/SgnCyc.lean:1007–1073](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/060c74468ebd536b7d5193975146e2b46c78f250/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/GaloisLibrary/FerreroWashingtonIndex/SgnCyc.lean#L1007-L1073) produces an explicit real cyclotomic unit for every odd c coprime to M. With k=(c-1)/2 it is

    u_c=zeta^(-k) (1+zeta+...+zeta^(c-1)).

The geometric sum is a unit because c is coprime to M. At an embedding zeta->exp(2 pi i t/M), t in [1,M-1], its value is sin(pi c t/M)/sin(pi t/M), a nonzero real number. The denominator is positive and the numerator sign is (-1)^floor(c t/M); therefore the corresponding signature is floor(c val(t)/M) mod 2. This is the actual floor-sign calculation, not an assumed arbitrary signature distribution.

The map kap:Z[G]->V sends coefficients of (1+J)x to their reductions modulo 2; its image is all conjugation-even functions because J has no fixed points on G (J=-1 nontrivial). [FerreroWashingtonIndex/SgnCyc.lean:783–858](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/060c74468ebd536b7d5193975146e2b46c78f250/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/GaloisLibrary/FerreroWashingtonIndex/SgnCyc.lean#L783-L858) proves I=kap^-1(S) is an ideal: translations preserve S, and sums and integer multiples preserve it. The signature of the actual cyclotomic unit and the constant sign of -1 show thetaH lies in I via thetaFull's coefficient identity. Also 1+J lies in I since (1+J)^2=2(1+J), and 2 lies in I. Thus the previously proved uniform relation (gamma-1)^N in (thetaH,1+J,2) implies multiplication by gamma-1 is nilpotent of exponent N on W=Z[G]/I. This quotient is finite, killed by 2 and has size [V:S]. Its further quotient by gamma-1 is generated by one image of a group basis element per coset of <gamma>, hence has size <=2^[G:<gamma>]. The previously audited nilCard gives

    [E:D] <= [V:S] = #W <= (2^[G:<gamma>])^N.

[FerreroWashingtonIndex/SgnCyc.lean:1293–1319](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/060c74468ebd536b7d5193975146e2b46c78f250/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/GaloisLibrary/FerreroWashingtonIndex/SgnCyc.lean#L1293-L1319) supplies every condition of this argument from the explicit conductor, unit, inertia and signature results. This is the precise provider used by pTwo. No division by 2, no semisimple decomposition in characteristic 2, and no use of the odd reflection theorem at 2.

## 4. A small residual from the Sinnott argument closed

[FerreroWashingtonIndex/EvenTot.lean:73–135](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/060c74468ebd536b7d5193975146e2b46c78f250/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/GaloisLibrary/FerreroWashingtonIndex/EvenTot.lean#L73-L135) selects exactly one representative from each {r,-r} pair of the finite root-of-unity subgroup of Z_l^×. If integers a,b satisfy ar=br' with a nonzero, raise to the common root order m>0 (even). This gives a^m=b^m in Z, hence b=±a. Cancellation then forces r'=±r, contradicting the choice of distinct representatives. This establishes the precise integer-multiple independence used by sinLem; it is not Q_l-linear independence. Reduction is injective on that torsion subgroup at all levels n>=2 by the teich comparison at ee(l). The latter provider and the exact gcShift/twCoef arithmetic identities still merit separate inspection.
