## 1. The exact sequence and Sha pairing are derived, not supplied as fields

[PoitouTateNineTermGs.lean:1468–1516](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/03553614b46e09b38af71ac1a9857cbfb5f9c150/src/GroundTheory/ProfiniteCohomology/PoitouTate/PoitouTateNineTermGs.lean#L1468-L1516) proves duality, Sha perfectness and the nine-term sequence from gsPoitouTateDualityHypotheses and gsBaseCase. The coefficient M is finite and discrete, killed by N != 0, and S contains every finite place dividing N. N odd or K totally complex handles the real-place modification.

[ZmodToQZCompactCohomologyResMapOfLocalInv.lean:91–113](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/03553614b46e09b38af71ac1a9857cbfb5f9c150/src/GroundTheory/ProfiniteCohomology/PoitouTate/ZmodToQZCompactCohomologyResMapOfLocalInv.lean#L91-L113) lists six ingredients: the cyclic underlying additive dualizing coefficient; perfect compact duality; global finiteness; compact finiteness; global degree-three vanishing; the trace compatibility with the sum of local invariants; and local pairing surjectivity (counting the two finiteness conditions separately, seven fields). In particular neither nine-term exactness nor Sha perfectness is a field.

Write G=G_S, M^D=Hom(M,mu_N), P^i=product H^i(K_v,M), and C^i=H_c^i(G,M). The localization-cone long exact sequence is

    ... -> H^i(G,M) -> P^i -> C^(i+1) -> H^(i+1)(G,M) -> ...

The compact duality map for the dual coefficient, transported through finite biduality, identifies C^(i+1) with Hom(H^(2-i)(G,M^D), Q/Z). Lines 125–160 implement this using the actual double-dual evaluation and cup naturality, not cardinality alone. Lines 178–184 check that the connecting local-to-compact map followed by this equivalence is the sum of local cup evaluations, using the trace compatibility. Lines 188–203 transport all three consecutive exactness identities through the additive equivalence. Lines 164–173 and 207–225 establish the left and right endpoints from H^3(G,M^D)=0 and H^3(G,M)=0 respectively. Lines 229–247 assemble the nine-term sequence.

Sha^1(M) is the image of C^1(M) in H^1(M); the ambiguity in a lift is the image of P^0(M). On the dual side Sha^2(M^D) is the kernel of localization. The proof at [251–302](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/03553614b46e09b38af71ac1a9857cbfb5f9c150/src/GroundTheory/ProfiniteCohomology/PoitouTate/ZmodToQZCompactCohomologyResMapOfLocalInv.lean#L251-L302) uses extension of Q/Z-valued characters from a subgroup (divisibility/injectivity of Q/Z), the perfect compact pairing, and local Tate pairing surjectivity to show that the quotient of C^1 by the ambiguity is exactly dual to Sha^2. Finiteness yields perfectness in the reverse direction. This is stronger evidence than the abstract isomorphism in the exposed ShaDual interface.

## 2. Construction of the trace

[BaseDefs.lean:344–382](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/03553614b46e09b38af71ac1a9857cbfb5f9c150/src/GroundTheory/ProfiniteCohomology/RestrictedRamification/BaseDefs.lean#L344-L382) constructs gsCompactInv, rather than postulating it. The global sum of local invariants is zero by reciprocity; localization followed by local-to-compact is exact, and local-to-compact in degree two is surjective because global H^3(G,mu_N) vanishes. Consequently the sum descends uniquely to a trace C^3(mu_N) -> Z/N. This argument alone does NOT imply bijectivity of the trace; BaseDualityHypotheses.bijective_alt is a separate condition. The global reciprocity and cohomological dimension results underlying this construction still merit review.

## 3. Arithmetic nondegeneracy for n=l^k

[PoitouTateNineTermGs.lean:1089–1138,1236–1242](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/03553614b46e09b38af71ac1a9857cbfb5f9c150/src/GroundTheory/ProfiniteCohomology/PoitouTate/PoitouTateNineTermGs.lean#L1089-L1138) proves the concrete cup identity: if d is a GsUnitsDatum, z is a character cocycle and y_d is its compact Kummer connecting class, then the trace of y_d cup z equals evaluation of the class field character on the S-idele class of d. The body chooses a local preimage of the compact class, compares the roots-to-units map, uses cone exactness, local cup reciprocity and the divisor character formula. It is an actual derivation of the identity, but the individual cochain identity gsRootsToUnitsCompact_cup_eq and sum_localInvU_eq_neg_divChar have not been fully audited.

[BaseClassIdele.lean:323–373](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/03553614b46e09b38af71ac1a9857cbfb5f9c150/src/GroundTheory/ProfiniteCohomology/RestrictedRamification/BaseClassIdele.lean#L323-L373) supplies two crucial lemmas. If the idele class of d is zero, its compact units class is a boundary. For any S-idele class a there exist m coprime to l and datum d with class(d)=m a. The latter splits the finite S-class group order into its l-power and prime-to-l parts, chooses a principal divisor for the resulting l-power multiple, and uses exists_divAbove_eq. This is not a claim that every idele class already has a datum without multiplication.

[ClassFieldGSFull.lean:447–520,588–615](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/03553614b46e09b38af71ac1a9857cbfb5f9c150/src/GroundTheory/ProfiniteCohomology/RestrictedRamification/ClassFieldGSFull.lean#L447-L520) constructs the class-field character correspondence, including the direction from abstract S-idele characters to continuous Galois characters: the kernel contains n-th powers and outside-S positive units, is open of finite index, and the class field existence theorem yields a finite abelian extension unramified outside S. The quotient topology then ensures continuity. The later odd/totally complex condition removes the positive-real-place distinction. This avoids silently identifying arbitrary abstract characters with continuous ones.

The two kernel proofs at [1255–1351](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/03553614b46e09b38af71ac1a9857cbfb5f9c150/src/GroundTheory/ProfiniteCohomology/PoitouTate/PoitouTateNineTermGs.lean#L1255-L1351) have distinct arithmetic content.

Left kernel: a nonzero H^1(G,Z/n) class gives a nontrivial continuous character, hence a nontrivial S-idele character by class field theory. Choose a class a detected by it and d with class(d)=m a, gcd(m,l)=1. If every compact pairing vanishes, the cup identity gives m chi(a)=0 in Z/n, impossible. The proof uses invertibility of m modulo l^k, not merely m != 0.

Right kernel: every compact H_c^2(mu_n) class comes from the compact Kummer connecting map and a units datum, by [BaseKummerDelta.lean:82–92](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/03553614b46e09b38af71ac1a9857cbfb5f9c150/src/GroundTheory/ProfiniteCohomology/RestrictedRamification/BaseKummerDelta.lean#L82-L92) and gsUnitsData_surjective. If y pairs trivially with every character then every Z/n-valued character of the S-idele class group annihilates class(d). Characters separate points of A/nA, so class(d)=n b. Choose d' with class(d')=m b, gcd(m,l)=1. Then class(m d - n d')=0, making its compact units class zero. Applying the connecting map gives m y=0 since n kills the roots-of-unity compact class. Also n y=0. Bezout gives y=0. The code handles n=1 separately (1309–1313). Both kernel arguments match the conventional finite-exponent character separation argument. The proof of character separation itself and the finite cardinality arguments still require independent kernel or source verification.

Why the Kummer connecting map is surjective matters: [BaseKummer.lean:269–311](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/03553614b46e09b38af71ac1a9857cbfb5f9c150/src/GroundTheory/ProfiniteCohomology/RestrictedRamification/BaseKummer.lean#L269-L311) uses injectivity on l-primary S-units Brauer classes, the global Brauer local-global principle, vanishing of real-place l-primary terms under the stated hypothesis, vanishing outside S, and local Hilbert 90. Hence H_c^2(S-units) has no l-primary torsion, forcing the roots-to-units map from n-torsion to be zero. This proves surjectivity via the compact long exact sequence, not by assuming it. The deeper theorem eq_zero_of_sUnitsBrauer_eq_zero and the splitting-field construction remain high-priority arithmetic checks.

## 4. From trivial coefficients to arbitrary finite modules

[PoincarePairs/Devissage.lean:178–206,244–274](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/03553614b46e09b38af71ac1a9857cbfb5f9c150/src/GroundTheory/ProfiniteCohomology/PoincarePairs/Devissage.lean#L178-L274) embeds every finite n-torsion discrete module M into a finite sum I of coinduced Z/n representations. Choose an open subgroup W <= U0 that fixes M. Every additive character M -> Z/n is W-equivariant; the induced coinduction maps separate points by evaluation at the identity. Thus 0 -> M -> I -> Q -> 0 is short exact, finite and n-torsion. Dualizing with the cyclic dualizing module is exact within the n-torsion category; this is not assuming Z/n is injective as an arbitrary Z-module.

The proof uses induction on cohomological degree, not induction on the size of Q (Q could be larger than M). At degree zero, injectivity follows from injectivity on H^0 and duality for I. Surjectivity uses the vanishing of H_c^4(Q^D), exactness of the two rows, and the already established degree-zero injectivity for Q. At degree r+1, lines 357–395 use the previous degree-r bijection for all modules and the signed compatibility of connecting maps; lines 399–443 first lift through I, then correct by a connecting class from Q. Lines 471–487 explicitly construct the four degree cases. [PoincarePairs.lean:197–276](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/03553614b46e09b38af71ac1a9857cbfb5f9c150/src/GroundTheory/ProfiniteCohomology/PoincarePairs.lean#L197-L276) supplies the coinduction comparison; [PoitouTateNineTermGs.lean:1383–1435](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/03553614b46e09b38af71ac1a9857cbfb5f9c150/src/GroundTheory/ProfiniteCohomology/PoitouTate/PoitouTateNineTermGs.lean#L1383-L1435) chooses U0 fixing roots of unity and supplies base pairing inputs for all relevant open subgroups. Corestriction in degree three is surjective by a separate theorem, not by assuming the extension degree is invertible. The exact Mackey/corestriction trace comparison in gsBaseBijective_of_le is a remaining high-priority bridge.

Finally [ZmodToQZCompactCohomologyResMapOfLocalInv.lean:854–883](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/03553614b46e09b38af71ac1a9857cbfb5f9c150/src/GroundTheory/ProfiniteCohomology/PoitouTate/ZmodToQZCompactCohomologyResMapOfLocalInv.lean#L854-L883) fills every field of the abstract hypothesis package. It combines prime-power dualities using inclusion of roots of unity and compatibility of compact traces, and separately supplies finiteness, dimension vanishing and local duality. No use of the final nine-term theorem is visible in these inspected provider bodies.
