# Poitou–Tate duality: a continuous mathematical reconstruction of the audited Lean proof

Snapshot 513a2af7e483b2770847d53561e947ec8f970a57. This is a human-readable proof of the main deduction from explicitly identified arithmetic and homological inputs. It is not a self-contained proof of all of class field theory, nor a certification of the full Lean kernel dependency graph. The accompanying pilot notes examine the arithmetic providers in more detail. The order below follows mathematical dependence instead of file order.

## 1. Statement and conventions

Let F be a number field and S a finite set of finite places containing the primes dividing a positive integer N. Assume N is odd or F is totally imaginary. Write G=Gal(F_S/F), where F_S is the maximal extension unramified at finite places outside S. Let M be a finite discrete G-module killed by N and put D=mu_N(F_S), M^D=Hom(M,D) with its contragredient G-action. Define H^i(M)=H^i(G,M), P^i(M)=product_{v in S} H^i(F_v,M), and A^*=Hom(A,Q/Z) for a finite abelian group A. We use the classical arithmetic-Frobenius Brauer invariant at this stage, not the sign-adjusted invariant of the downstream Published.PoitouTateNormalised interface.

For the finite-place localization cochain map f, let C_c^i(M)=C^i(G,M) ⊕ product_v C^{i-1}(F_v,M), with differential (a,b) |-> (da, f(a)-db). The local part in degree zero is zero. This is a shifted cone. The condition on N and F removes modified archimedean Tate terms. It does not assert that ordinary archimedean H^0 vanishes.

We prove the perfect compact pairing H^r(M) x H_c^{3-r}(M^D) -> Q/Z, the nine-term localization sequence, and the perfect Sha pairing. Here the actual compact cup is written with the compact argument first, followed by the global argument, as in compactDualityMap. Signs in alternative orders must be tracked separately.

## 2. Arithmetic inputs (not consequences of the desired duality)

(A) Local Tate duality: H^r(F_v,M) and H^{2-r}(F_v,M^D) are finite and in perfect duality via the normalized local invariant. H^i(F_v,M)=0 for i>=3.

(B) Restricted global dimension and finiteness: for all finite N-torsion M, H^i(M) is finite and vanishes for i>=3. The cone long exact sequence then gives finite compact groups and H_c^4(M)=0. The vanishing uses P^3(M)=0 and H^4(M)=0; it is independent of global duality.

(C) For n=l^k dividing N, the primary S-unit Brauer comparison identifies the l-primary part of H²(G,O_{F_S,S}^×) with the finite-support primary local invariant tuples supported on S (and allowed infinite places) having total sum zero. H³ of the S-unit module has no l-primary part. Kummer exactness for the S-units follows from the unramifiedness of adjoining roots whose degree is invertible outside S.

(D) Class field theory gives the isomorphism between continuous characters G->Z/n and all abstract S-idele class characters to Z/n (continuity on the Galois side). The actual charEquiv in ClassFieldGSFull.lean:610–615 has ordinary MonoidHom on the idelic side. The compact S-unit datum results used below are: every H_c^1(S-units) class has a datum d=(t,(a_v)); if its idele class c(d) is zero then its compact class is zero; for every S-idele class a there is a datum with c(d)=m a in additive notation for some m coprime to l. The latter does NOT assert unconditional surjectivity of the datum map. For any abelian group A, its quotient A/nA is detected by abstract homomorphisms to Z/n: a nonzero element in the quotient can be detected by a character because Z/n is injective in the n-torsion category. The proof uses this general separation lemma, exists_nsmul_eq_of_forall_zmod, not finiteness of A/nA.

## 3. Construct the compact trace

First take n=l^k, k>=1, and D_n=mu_n. By (B), P²(D_n)->H_c³(D_n) is surjective. Global reciprocity makes the sum of the local invariants zero on the image of H²(D_n). Hence the sum descends uniquely to T:H_c³(D_n)->Z/n, where Z/n is identified with the n-torsion in Q/Z by a |-> a/n.

Surjectivity of T follows by selecting a finite prime in S above l and prescribing a local invariant there, zero elsewhere. For injectivity, take a local tuple xi in the kernel of its invariant sum. By (C), its image in the local Brauer groups is realized by an l-primary S-unit H² class y. The n-multiple of y has zero Brauer image, since xi is n-torsion. Primary injection gives n y=0. The Kummer sequence therefore lifts y to H²(G,D_n). By local Kummer injectivity its restrictions are xi, so xi is zero in compact cohomology. This proves that T is an isomorphism.

## 4. Establish the arithmetic degree-one pairing over fields containing mu_n

Assume now mu_n is fixed by G. Put U_S=O_{F_S,S}^×. The compact Kummer map delta:H_c¹(U_S)->H_c²(D_n) is surjective: the n-torsion of H_c²(U_S) vanishes. Indeed the image of a primary compact class in global H²(U_S) has zero local Brauer components inside S by cone exactness, outside S by the S-unit condition, and at infinity by the odd/complex hypothesis. Global Brauer injection makes this image zero. The class then comes from local H¹ of the multiplicative groups, zero by Hilbert 90. This is the proof of the compact torsion statement, not an appeal to Poitou–Tate.

For a datum d as in (D) and a global character z in H¹(Z/n), let chi be its S-idele class character. The essential identity, in Q/Z, is

    T(delta(d) cup z) = chi(c(d)),

with T and chi both scaled into Q/Z. It follows from the actual Kummer/Bockstein product identity. To see the sign explicitly, let b(g) in [0,n-1] lift z(g), and B(g,h)=(b(g)+b(h)-b(gh))/n. In the compact cone, write the global cocycle of d as dm with m=t in the larger units module. Then dm cup B is n-torsion in H³(U_S); by (C) choose q with dq=dm cup B. The global two-cocycle W=m cup B-q in the larger units module replaces the global part of the compact cup after subtracting the cone coboundary of (q,0). Locally this yields res(W)+a_v cup B. At primes outside S, the valuation of W is ord_v(t)B; the unramified valuation comparison and local reciprocity give precisely the outside-S divisor character. Reciprocity for the global correction class shows that the sum of the inside-S contributions from W is the NEGATIVE of that divisor character. If n is odd, only the sum of the infinite-place contributions must vanish: it is killed by 2 and by n. The local a_v cup B terms supply the positive local character values. The result is evaluation on the idele presentation with local entries a_v and outside-S divisor -div(t), exactly c(d).

This proves both kernels are zero. If z is nonzero, choose an idele class a with chi(a) nonzero, using the character isomorphism. Take d with c(d)=m a, gcd(m,l)=1. Then chi(c(d)) is nonzero, so z is detected. Conversely, suppose y in H_c²(D_n) pairs to zero with every z. Choose a datum d with delta(d)=y. Then all abstract Z/n characters kill c(d), by the actual charEquiv, hence c(d)=n a for some idele class a. Choose d' with c(d')=m a, gcd(m,l)=1. The datum m d-n d' has zero idele class, so its compact S-unit class is zero. Applying delta gives m y=0 (the target is killed by n). Since gcd(m,n)=1, y=0. Both groups are finite by (B); the two kernel statements give a perfect pairing.

## 5. From degree one to all degrees for the trivial cyclic coefficient

Write A=Z/n. Since D_n is trivial cyclic, choose an equivariant D_n≅A. Degree zero pairs H⁰(A)=A with H_c³(D_n) by T and scalar multiplication. Degree three pairs two zero groups: H³(A)=0 and H_c⁰(D_n)=0 (localization is injective on invariants, since S is nonempty). Degree one was proved above.

For degree two, suppose x in H²(A) annihilates H_c¹(D_n). Test against local-to-compact images of P⁰(D_n), one place at a time. Local duality implies res_v x=0 for every v. Lift x to xc in H_c²(A). By the compact cup swap identity in degrees 1 and 2, the functional y |-> T(xc cup y) on H¹(D_n) vanishes on the kernel of localization, because classes in that kernel lift to H_c¹(D_n). It thus descends to the image in P¹(D_n), and extends to the whole product: Z/n is injective as a module over itself, or equivalently extend into Q/Z and note the ambient group is n-torsion. By local duality realize this extended character as a local tuple in P¹(A). The signed local cup formula permits adding its local-to-compact image to xc so that the functional vanishes on all of H¹(D_n). The already proved degree-one pairing, transported through D_n≅A, forces the corrected compact class to vanish. Its global image is still x, so x=0.

For surjectivity, start with a functional on H_c¹(D_n). Restrict to the image of P⁰(D_n); local duality gives a tuple xi in P²(A) representing the restriction. Its pairing with the diagonal invariant generator of D_n is zero, by cone exactness. Therefore its sum of invariants (after A≅D_n) is zero. The trace isomorphism implies xi is in the image of H²(A); choose a lift x0. Subtract the functional represented by x0. The remaining functional vanishes on the local-to-compact image, hence factors through the image in H¹(D_n); extend it to H¹(D_n). The perfect degree-one pairing supplies a compact H_c²(A) class realizing this extended functional (using the compact cup swap). Its image in H²(A) corrects x0 to the desired class. This proves perfectness in degree two.

## 6. Pass to coinduced modules

Choose an open subgroup U0 fixing D_n, and any open U contained in U0. Its fixed field L contains mu_n; the primes above l remain in S_L, and the infinite-place hypothesis persists. The previous sections give the perfect trivial cyclic pairing over L.

The actual Mackey/Shapiro isomorphism carries compact cohomology of coinduced modules over F to compact cohomology over the local Mackey family at U. Its construction includes the homotopy correction (a,b)->(alpha a,beta b-h a) and exact compatibility with compact cups. The local double cosets correspond to the places w over S. Ordinary Shapiro is restriction and evaluation at 1. Dual of the coinduced module is transported by the coefficient dual isomorphism, and its evaluation pairing becomes the pointwise pairing followed by the coset trace.

To transport perfectness, the existing Lean proof does not need exact trace naturality: T_F cor is surjective by the following argument. The coefficient trace Coind Res D_n -> D_n is onto, since a function supported on U can have any desired trace. Its kernel is finite n-torsion. Compact H⁴ of the kernel is zero by (B), hence compact H³ of the trace is onto. Compact Shapiro then gives the desired surjectivity. Meanwhile T_L is bijective. On the cyclic H_c³ group the two maps differ by a unit in Z/n; multiplying by that unit preserves perfectness. Thus all coinduced A modules for U<=U0 have perfect compact pairings in every degree. The stronger exact trace formula is separately specified in pilot-02-exact-trace-spec.md and is NOT used as a new Lean assumption here.

## 7. Degree induction for all finite n-torsion modules

Every such M embeds into a finite direct sum I of coinduced trivial A modules from an open subgroup U contained in U0 fixing M. To construct the embedding, take enough additive characters M->A to separate points and use coinduction adjunction. The cokernel Q is again finite and n-torsion. Dualizing 0->M->I->Q->0 gives 0->Q^D->I^D->M^D->0, exact because Hom(-,D_n) is exact on n-torsion modules. This argument does not induct on the cardinality of M; Q may be larger.

Set A_r(M)=H^r(M) and B_r(M)=Hom(H_c^{3-r}(M^D),A). The coefficient evaluation and compact cup give natural maps A_r->B_r; the connecting arrows commute up to the invertible sign determined by the cup degree. In degree zero the diagram starts with zero on both sides (H_c⁴(Q^D)=0). At the following terms, use the known isomorphism for I, injectivity in degree zero for Q, and exactness to lift functionals. More explicitly, injectivity for every module in degree zero follows from embedding in I. Surjectivity then lifts the given functional to I; its image in Q is zero by that just-established injectivity; exactness lifts back to M, and the resulting functional agrees because H_c³(I^D)->H_c³(M^D) is onto.

Suppose duality in degree r holds for EVERY finite n-torsion module. First prove injectivity in degree r+1 for every M. If x maps to the zero functional, its image in I is zero by perfectness for I. Thus x=delta(q), q in H^r(Q). The functional represented by q vanishes on the kernel of H_c^{3-r}(Q^D)->H_c^{3-r}(I^D), by the signed connecting-map pairing identity. Extend it across that map to the compact group of I^D. Degree-r perfectness for I realizes the extension by v in H^r(I). Degree-r injectivity for Q then identifies q with the image of v. Hence delta(q)=0.

Having proved this injectivity for all M in degree r+1, prove surjectivity. Given a functional on H_c^{2-r}(M^D), pull it back to H_c^{2-r}(I^D) and represent by v in H^{r+1}(I). Its image in Q pairs to zero and hence vanishes by the newly proved injectivity for Q. Lift v to x' in H^{r+1}(M). The difference between the desired functional and the one represented by x' vanishes on the image from I^D. It factors through the compact connecting map into H_c^{3-r}(Q^D); extend it to that group and represent by q in H^r(Q). Add the signed correction delta(q) to x'. The signed pairing identity gives the desired functional. This proves the induction, for r+1<=3.

## 8. General N and the nine-term sequence

For every prime power n exactly dividing N, the root inclusion D_n->D_N identifies D_n with the n-torsion of D_N. Thus the dual coefficient of an n-torsion module can be computed with either. After embedding Z/n and Z/N into Q/Z, the traces commute with this inclusion because they are sums of the same normalized Brauer invariants. Decompose the coefficient by the coprime primary components using Bezout idempotents. Cup, duals and cohomology respect this decomposition, and cross-prime pairings vanish. Hence the perfect pairings for all primary modules give the result for M. The zero module case N=1 is trivial. [ZmodToQZCompactCohomologyResMapOfLocalInv.lean:515–571,791–880](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/513a2af7e483b2770847d53561e947ec8f970a57/src/GroundTheory/ProfiniteCohomology/PoitouTate/ZmodToQZCompactCohomologyResMapOfLocalInv.lean#L515-L571) implements this reduction.

Apply compact duality also to M^D and use finite biduality to identify H_c^{i+1}(M) with H^{2-i}(M^D)^*. The cone long exact sequence and compatibility of the local-to-compact arrow with the local cup pairing now give

    0 -> H⁰(M) -> P⁰(M) -> H²(M^D)^*
      -> H¹(M) -> P¹(M) -> H¹(M^D)^*
      -> H²(M) -> P²(M) -> H⁰(M^D)^* -> 0.

The first zero follows from H_c⁰(M)=0 by duality and H³(M^D)=0; the last follows from H³(M)=0. This is the nine-term result, with local arrows given by sum of local pairings and the other arrows transported from the cone. The source uses these actual maps, not just equal group orders.

## 9. Sha perfectness as a quotient of compact duality

Let Sha^i(M)=ker(H^i(M)->P^i(M)). For x in Sha¹(M), lift x to xc in H_c¹(M), and pair with y in Sha²(M^D) via compact duality. The pairing is independent of the lift because differences come from P⁰(M), and y has zero localization. If x pairs to zero with Sha², the character on H²(M^D) defined by xc factors through the localization image. Extend it to P²(M^D); local duality represents it by a tuple in P⁰(M). Compact duality injectivity implies xc is the local-to-compact image of that tuple, so x=0. For surjectivity, extend a character on Sha² to H²(M^D) by injectivity of Q/Z; compact duality represents it by xc. The global image of xc is in Sha¹, and its induced pairing gives the original character. The groups are finite, so the flipped pairing is also perfect.

## 10. Boundary to the downstream normalized interface

The theorem just derived uses the classical Brauer invariant. The downstream Published.PoitouTateNormalised interface specializes to prime-power coefficients and uses the negative classical invariant, identified by the Kummer-first cocycle normalization on arithmetic Frobenius. Its middle localization arrow uses the reversed degree-one cup order. Thus relative to classical primal-first cups the three arrow signs are (-,+,-); uniform sign changes preserve exactness. The downstream Sha statement records abstract duality isomorphisms rather than the full canonical pairing. The local decomposition/completion comparison and actual signed transport are audited in pilot-02-normalization-sign.md and pilot-02-provider-bridge.md. This reconstruction is of the Poitou–Tate component, not a proof of the Riemann hypothesis.
