## 1. S-unit injection is only a primary assertion

Write F_S for the maximal extension unramified outside the finite primes S (infinite places not restricted here), G=Gal(F_S/F), U=O_(F_S,S)^×, L=F_S^×, and P=L/U. Let D be the outside-S divisor module and C=D/P the corresponding divisor quotient at the infinite level. All are discrete G-modules; use additive notation for the group calculations.

[RestrictedRamification/Brauer.lean:205–232](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/2fe54dae5a956978e1f1b07a791031fbab7c266a/src/GroundTheory/ProfiniteCohomology/RestrictedRamification/Brauer.lean#L205-L232) proves injectivity of H²(G,U) -> H²(G,L) on l-primary torsion, for l prime with all places above l in S. From 0 -> U -> L -> P -> 0, a class y in the kernel is the connecting image of p in H¹(G,P). Hilbert 90 for the Galois extension F_S/F gives H¹(G,L)=0, so the connecting map is injective. Thus if l^k y=0 then l^k p=0. From 0 -> P -> D -> C -> 0, H¹(G,D)=0 (the integral divisor module has the appropriate coinduced description), so p comes from H⁰(G,C). Each element of C is killed by an integer coprime to l, as shown below; the same applies to H⁰, and hence p is zero by Bezout. The proof does not claim the whole divisor quotient vanishes or assume that the finite-level class number is coprime to l.

[RestrictedRamification/BrauerSplit.lean:73–132,172–192](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/2fe54dae5a956978e1f1b07a791031fbab7c266a/src/GroundTheory/ProfiniteCohomology/RestrictedRamification/BrauerSplit.lean#L73-L132) proves injectivity of inflation H²(G,L) -> Br(F). It descends a continuous cocycle to a finite normal field, compares its embeddings in the two ambient fields, and invokes finite-level inflation injectivity (the conventional Hilbert-90 inflation-restriction argument). [Brauer.lean:337–344](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/2fe54dae5a956978e1f1b07a791031fbab7c266a/src/GroundTheory/ProfiniteCohomology/RestrictedRamification/Brauer.lean#L337-L344) composes the two injections, giving precisely the primary injection needed by the compact Kummer proof. The finite-level inflation providers remain to be independently reviewed.

## 2. Why the infinite-level divisor quotient has no l-primary obstruction

[RestrictedRamification/BrauerDivisor.lean:590–742,748–771](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/2fe54dae5a956978e1f1b07a791031fbab7c266a/src/GroundTheory/ProfiniteCohomology/RestrictedRamification/BrauerDivisor.lean#L590-L771) proves the relevant capitulation result by a construction rather than class-number assumptions. At a finite normal level L/F and a place W above a prime outside S, take a principal element a with divisor h_L W, where h_L is the finite class number. Factor h_L=l^j m with gcd(m,l)=1. Take an l^j-th root b of a. Every finite valuation of a is divisible by l^j; outside S the residue characteristic is prime to l. Hence L(b)/L and L(b)/F are unramified outside the respective S (using the already unramified L/F and tower compatibility). In the outside-S divisor module b has valuation m at the places over W and zero at the others. Thus m times the characteristic divisor attached to W becomes principal in F_S. The source checks the coset description of the primes using decomposition groups (650–723), then extends from finite-level characteristic functions to arbitrary finite-support locally constant divisor functions (748–771). It is a prime-to-l annihilation statement, not literal capitulation of every divisor.

The necessary Kummer ramification lemma is [GlobalFields/KummerTheory/Unramified.lean:103–166](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/2fe54dae5a956978e1f1b07a791031fbab7c266a/src/GroundTheory/GlobalFields/KummerTheory/Unramified.lean#L103-L166). If alpha^N=a, residue characteristic does not divide N and N divides the valuation of a, rescale alpha by a base-field element to make the new generator an integral unit at the chosen prime. Its minimal polynomial divides X^N-b; the derivative identity at the generator gives f'(alpha)g(alpha)=N alpha^(N-1), a unit at that prime. Since f'(alpha) belongs to the different, the prime does not divide the different. This proves unramifiedness for the simple extension even without assuming all N-th roots of unity in the base field. [BrauerDivisor.lean:88–139](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/2fe54dae5a956978e1f1b07a791031fbab7c266a/src/GroundTheory/ProfiniteCohomology/RestrictedRamification/BrauerDivisor.lean#L88-L139) checks the passage back to the maximal unramified-outside-S field. [SDivisor.lean:472–534](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/2fe54dae5a956978e1f1b07a791031fbab7c266a/src/GroundTheory/ProfiniteCohomology/RestrictedRamification/SDivisor.lean#L472-L534) separately uses this lemma for the exact divisor lifting needed by the idele datum argument in the preceding audit.

## 3. Brauer local-global injectivity and restricted splitting

[GlobalClassFormation/EqZeroOfBrauerLocalApplyEqZero.lean:89–113](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/2fe54dae5a956978e1f1b07a791031fbab7c266a/src/GroundTheory/ProfiniteCohomology/GlobalClassFormation/EqZeroOfBrauerLocalApplyEqZero.lean#L89-L113) constructs the localization map by the units-to-ideles map followed by the degree-two idele cohomology equivalence, and proves injectivity using H¹ of the idele class module equal to zero. This matches the classical derivation from 1 -> Fbar^× -> J_Fbar -> C_Fbar -> 1. [ClassLevels.lean:179–209](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/2fe54dae5a956978e1f1b07a791031fbab7c266a/src/GroundTheory/ProfiniteCohomology/GlobalClassFormation/ClassLevels.lean#L179-L209) represents continuous cohomology classes at finite Galois levels, then applies finite-level H¹(Gal(L/F),C_L)=0. [NatCardGroupCohomologyTwoIdeleClassRepDvd.lean:614–627](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/2fe54dae5a956978e1f1b07a791031fbab7c266a/src/GroundTheory/ProfiniteCohomology/GlobalClassFormation/NatCardGroupCohomologyTwoIdeleClassRepDvd.lean#L614-L627) proves the latter via Sylow restrictions and p-group vanishing, with tower inflation-restriction at 165–184. This is recognizable class formation reasoning; the cyclic base case and the idele cohomology equivalence are separate residual checks.

An arbitrary l^k-torsion Brauer class is split inside F_S, not merely in some finite extension. [BrauerSplit.lean:412–469](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/2fe54dae5a956978e1f1b07a791031fbab7c266a/src/GroundTheory/ProfiniteCohomology/RestrictedRamification/BrauerSplit.lean#L412-L469) first adjoins l²-th roots of unity to make the field totally imaginary (including l=2). It restricts the class, forms the finite support of its local components, and chooses a cyclotomic layer whose completion degree at every prime in that support is divisible by l^k. [GlobalClassFormation/Splitting.lean:206–255](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/2fe54dae5a956978e1f1b07a791031fbab7c266a/src/GroundTheory/ProfiniteCohomology/GlobalClassFormation/Splitting.lean#L206-L255) proves the resulting restriction vanishes: local invariant multiplies by the completion degree; the torsion class is killed locally, then the Brauer localization injection kills it globally. The use of finite support and divisibility, rather than merely unbounded degree, is essential.

[RestrictedRamification/BrauerSplitLayers.lean:91–190](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/2fe54dae5a956978e1f1b07a791031fbab7c266a/src/GroundTheory/ProfiniteCohomology/RestrictedRamification/BrauerSplitLayers.lean#L91-L190) proves the divisibility condition for arbitrary nonzero m. For each prime q dividing m and each specified finite place over rational p, the relevant decomposition subgroup has a nonzero q-adic cyclotomic degree component. For p=q it uses a generator of the principal cyclotomic unit component; for p != q it uses arithmetic Frobenius p, whose q-adic logarithm component is nonzero since p has infinite order modulo the torsion subgroup of Z_q^×. Raising to the finite index of the normal core of the global fixing subgroup keeps this component nonzero by torsion-freeness. Taking a maximum over the finite set of primes and places bounds its detection level; reduction modulo a sufficiently large power gives an element of order divisible by q^v_q(m). Since the degree of a completion is the order of the corresponding decomposition group, m divides the degree. The code explicitly treats all places above v and handles the empty support and m=1 by vacuous quantification. Independent checking of the stabilizer/mod-character lemmas and the q-adic logarithm implementation is still needed.

The layer embeds in F(mu_(l^b)), which is unramified outside l at finite primes. The source uses this containment, the condition l in S, and transitivity of restriction. Finally [BrauerSplit.lean:351–366](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/2fe54dae5a956978e1f1b07a791031fbab7c266a/src/GroundTheory/ProfiniteCohomology/RestrictedRamification/BrauerSplit.lean#L351-L366) uses degree-two inflation-restriction and Hilbert 90 to descend the split class from the finite Galois quotient to H²(G,F_S^×). It does not need the whole algebraic closure as the restricted coefficient field.

## 4. Global class field existence provider

[GlobalFields/ExistenceTheorem/ExistsIsAbelianGaloisRangeIdeleClassNormEqOfFini.lean](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/2fe54dae5a956978e1f1b07a791031fbab7c266a/src/GroundTheory/GlobalFields/ExistenceTheorem/ExistsIsAbelianGaloisRangeIdeleClassNormEqOfFini.lean) proves the open finite-index norm correspondence used in the preceding nondegeneracy audit.

For a field containing mu_n, lines 252–279 prove that the norm group of the S-unit Kummer extension equals the image of the relevant idelic power subgroup. Inclusion uses local norm-residue symbols; equality uses the global norm index and an explicit matching finite index. Lines 456–479 enlarge S so the power subgroup is contained in the prescribed open subgroup N and then take the fixed field of its image under reciprocity, using lines 310–334. For a general field, lines 774–795 adjoin the roots of unity of order [C_F:N], pull N back along the norm, use the roots-of-unity case, and descend by norm limitation. Lines 625–644 explicitly prove equality of the norm group for a finite Galois extension and its maximal abelian subextension using the index bound by the abelianization order and norm transitivity. Lines 846–859 embed the resulting abelian field into the chosen algebraic closure. Thus the existence theorem is not simply postulated at the place where the character correspondence is constructed. The inspected argument relies on global reciprocity, the norm limitation index bound and the Kummer index computations; these are important residual proof providers, not certified by this note.
