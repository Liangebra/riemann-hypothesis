## A. Lemma K: geometry and group homology

### A1. The precise abstract homological bound

[TorsionBoundsChains/FaceDSingle.lean:55–132](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/94a362452b3d23ee6172e7b418ad183433030ac6/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/TorsionBoundsChains/FaceDSingle.lean#L55-L132) defines TorsLe to require finiteness of the torsion subgroup AND a cardinality bound; CardLe requires finiteness of the whole object. Let G act on sets X_p with an equivariant augmented chain complex of free permutation modules Z[X_p], exact through the required degree. Suppose the orbit count of X_p for p<=m is at most A, all stabilizers there are finite of order <=s, and the boundary of each basis element in X_(m+1) has l1 norm <=b. The theorem [TorsionBoundsChains/LogLeExp.lean:528–581](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/94a362452b3d23ee6172e7b418ad183433030ac6/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/TorsionBoundsChains/LogLeExp.lean#L528-L581) gives

    #Tor H_m(G,Z) <= b^A s^(m A s^m),   s,b>=1.

The X_p themselves need not be finite. Nor does this assert H_m is finite. Exactness through degree m includes the appropriate last relation map from degree m+1. For the face differential b=m+2, since the alternating sum of m+2 faces may have repetitions, which can only reduce its l1 norm. This last boundary estimate is checked in L1MapDomainLeAndL1FaceDSingleLe.lean.

### A2. The base case is integer lattice torsion, not a bound on the entire quotient

[TorsionBoundsChains/LogLeExp.lean:347–450](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/94a362452b3d23ee6172e7b418ad183433030ac6/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/TorsionBoundsChains/LogLeExp.lean#L347-L450) treats the torsion of Z^a/span(L), a<=A, for a possibly infinite set of integral relations of l1 norm <=b. Choose a maximal independent family B0 in L and extend it by coordinate vectors to a full independent family B1. Its columns still have l1 norm <=b. The full-rank lattice Lambda=span_Z B1 has index |det(B1)|<=b^a by the determinant product estimate. For every torsion class choose a representative. If two chosen representatives differ by Lambda, the difference x is also in the rational span of L, which is the rational span of B0. Independence of B1 forces its coordinates outside B0 to vanish. Since x lies in Lambda, the remaining coordinates are integral, hence x lies in span_Z B0 subset span_Z L. Thus the chosen representatives inject the torsion classes into Z^a/Lambda. This is a set injection, not an asserted canonical quotient homomorphism. The source explicitly clears denominators and uses torsion-freeness (torsSat_coeff and torsSat_mult). The full quotient may be infinite.

The degree-zero homology argument passes to coinvariants: the permutation module becomes a free abelian group on orbits. Mapping a relation to the orbit set cannot increase its l1 norm. The same integer lattice argument applies to the coinvariant presentation. No cancellation of cardinalities of infinite modules occurs.

### A3. Positive degree: finite stabilizer homology and dimension shifting

[TorsionBoundsChains/PermCard.lean:412–494](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/94a362452b3d23ee6172e7b418ad183433030ac6/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/TorsionBoundsChains/PermCard.lean#L412-L494) proves for a finite group S, j>=1,

    #H_j(S,Z) <= |S|^(|S|^j).

The inhomogeneous j-chain module for trivial coefficients is free on S^j. Its subgroup of cycles has rank <=|S|^j. The positive-degree homology is killed by |S| (kill provider, still to inspect), so it is a quotient of the cycles modulo |S|. subquot implements that argument. Thus the bound includes genuine finiteness; the restriction j>=1 is essential (H_0(S,Z)=Z). A transitive permutation module is identified via Shapiro with stabilizer homology. The source then splits off orbits one at a time using exact sequences, proving

    #H_j(G,Z[X]) <= s^(A s^j), j>=1.

The Shapiro and positive-degree annihilation providers remain independent dependencies. Empty X, A=0 and s>=1 are explicitly handled.

For the augmented complex write K=ker(Z[X_0]->M), where M is the target module. The shifted resolution resolves K. The long exact sequence includes

    H_m(G,Z[X_0]) -> H_m(G,M) -> H_(m-1)(G,K).

For an exact sequence A'->B'->C' with A' finite and Tor C' finite, the map Tor B'->Tor C' has kernel contained in the image of A'; hence #Tor B'<=#A' #Tor C'. This is proved by actual kernel/range equivalences in [TorsionBoundsChains/LogLeExp.lean:469–519](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/94a362452b3d23ee6172e7b418ad183433030ac6/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/TorsionBoundsChains/LogLeExp.lean#L469-L519). Applying this to the above sequence (m>=1), and iterating dimension shifting, gives the sharper intermediate product b^A * s^(A sum_(j=1)^m s^j). The source bounds the exponent by m A s^m. No assertion that taking torsion is an exact functor is needed. This closes the abstract deduction from the permutation resolution and finite orbit/stabilizer bounds, modulo the stated lower-level providers.

### A4. The geometric nerve is acyclic by an actual chain argument

[HermiteNormalForms/SmulStrictMono.lean:129–155](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/94a362452b3d23ee6172e7b418ad183433030ac6/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/HermiteNormalForms/SmulStrictMono.lean#L129-L155) defines the positive Hermitian forms, the Ryshkov-type polyhedron Ry={h:q_h(y)>=1 for every nonzero integral vector y}, and sets of minimal vectors. The actual tuple complex used in tors_le is the nerve of normal cones at vertices, not an arbitrary contractible space declared to have finite orbit count.

[ConvexNerveComplexes/AcycSimp.lean:81–164](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/94a362452b3d23ee6172e7b418ad183433030ac6/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/ConvexNerveComplexes/AcycSimp.lean#L81-L164) proves local finiteness of relevant vertices on a compact region: local finiteness of the defining inequalities gives finitely many active constraints after taking a finite subcover. The active set determines a vertex uniquely because its evaluations separate the space of forms. For a finite set of positive dual functionals, any vertex minimizing a convex combination lies in the union of their compact sublevel sets relative to a fixed feasible h0. Indeed if all functionals are strictly larger at the vertex than at h0, the convex combination is too. This proves the required finiteness of normal cones meeting the convex hull of finitely many witnesses.

For a compact convex X covered by closed convex A_i, with only finitely many meeting X, [ConvexNerveComplexes/AcycSimp.lean:468–527](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/94a362452b3d23ee6172e7b418ad183433030ac6/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/ConvexNerveComplexes/AcycSimp.lean#L468-L527) chooses a maximal family of A_i with common intersection and another A_i disjoint from that intersection if there is no point common to all. Their intersections with X are nonempty disjoint compact convex sets. Strict separation provides a hyperplane f=u. [ConvexNerveComplexes/AcycSimp.lean:423–460](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/94a362452b3d23ee6172e7b418ad183433030ac6/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/ConvexNerveComplexes/AcycSimp.lean#L423-L460) verifies that the nerve of X is the union of the two cut nerves, and their intersection is the nerve on the hyperplane: for a tuple with witnesses on opposite sides, the segment between the witnesses lies in X and in every A_i for that tuple, and meets f=u. This is stronger than merely asserting that the two underlying spaces meet.

[ConvexNerveComplexes/AcycSimp.lean:551–608](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/94a362452b3d23ee6172e7b418ad183433030ac6/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/ConvexNerveComplexes/AcycSimp.lean#L551-L608) uses strong induction on the number of realizable finite face sets. If all relevant A_i share a point, adjoining one fixed vertex gives a cone contraction. Otherwise each cut nerve loses a specific realizable face: the lower cut loses the separated maximal family, the upper cut loses the separated singleton, the middle loses that maximal family. All three cuts are nonempty, including the middle by convexity. Their nerves are acyclic by induction. The union is acyclic by [ConvexNerveComplexes/AcycSimp.lean:377–418](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/94a362452b3d23ee6172e7b418ad183433030ac6/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/ConvexNerveComplexes/AcycSimp.lean#L377-L418): split a cycle as y+z supported in the two subcomplexes; dy=-dz belongs to the intersection complex; find e there with de=dy, then y-e and z+e are cycles and bound separately. Augmentation is handled separately. This is an explicit integral chain Mayer–Vietoris argument. It works for the actual ordered tuples, including repeats; it does not assume the complex of all ordered tuples is the ordinary simplicial chain complex without a comparison.

For the possibly noncompact convex positive dual, [ConvexNerveComplexes/AcycSimp.lean:617–667](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/94a362452b3d23ee6172e7b418ad183433030ac6/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/ConvexNerveComplexes/AcycSimp.lean#L617-L667) encloses any finite collection of simplex witnesses (and one base point) in their compact convex hull and applies the compact result, using the cone-finiteness property. Every chain has finite support; the finite-witness directed argument suffices for acyclicity. Assembly.lean:116–162 identifies these supported chains and their boundary maps with the actual free tuple modules. The geometric Good conditions for the Hermitian cone (positive definiteness, existence of a minimizer vertex, local finiteness and compact sublevels) remain separate substantive providers, not assumptions on arbitrary cones. They are supplied at HermiteNormalForms/Imports.lean:200ff; their proofs need a dedicated audit.

### A5. Finiteness of orbits and stabilizers

[HermiteNormalForms/ColsMinSmul.lean:232–295](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/94a362452b3d23ee6172e7b418ad183433030ac6/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/HermiteNormalForms/ColsMinSmul.lean#L232-L295) constructs, for every tuple of normal-cone vertices, a nonsingular integral matrix A whose columns are common minimal vectors. The common spanning condition follows from simp_span and spanpsd; the nonsingular columns from exbasis. The determinant norm bound nrmle permits replacing A by g A using the finite Hermite-normal-form representatives in [HermiteNormalForms/Hnf.lean:180–215](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/94a362452b3d23ee6172e7b418ad183433030ac6/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/HermiteNormalForms/Hnf.lean#L180-L215). For a fixed A, nearvx bounds the number of possible vertices via finite subsets of nearby lattice vectors: at most dd*N independent active evaluations determine a vertex (vxdet, [HermiteNormalForms/ColsMinSmul.lean:169–226](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/94a362452b3d23ee6172e7b418ad183433030ac6/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/HermiteNormalForms/ColsMinSmul.lean#L169-L226)). A tuple orbit injects into the pairs (representative matrix, tuple of nearby vertices). Equality of such pairs gives equality of the actual transformed tuples. The estimate counts ordered tuples and allows repetitions. The core determinant and nearby-vector estimates, HNF classification hnfone, and spanpsd/simp_span require further independent checking.

For stabilizers [HermiteNormalForms/ImportsAlt.lean:173–240](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/94a362452b3d23ee6172e7b418ad183433030ac6/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/HermiteNormalForms/ImportsAlt.lean#L173-L240) uses stabilizer inclusion into the stabilizer of the first vertex and an independent finiteness proof stabfin. For a finite subgroup of units in a finite free Z-algebra of rank d, reduction modulo 3 is injective: if h1=h2 mod 3, h2^-1 h1 lies in 1+3R, has finite order, and torsfree implies it is 1. Hence its order is <=3^d. In the matrix algebra over the integers of L, d=[L:Q] N²=dd*N. This is an injection into a finite additive residue set; no claim that every residue matrix is invertible. Residual: torsfree's binomial proof and stabfin's arithmetic geometry.

Putting A=orbBound, s=3^(dd*N), b=m+2 in the abstract theorem is exactly Assembly.lean:173–180. The earlier audit of torsAll then absorbs the explicit numerical factors into exponent 24. This now connects the geometric, homological and arithmetic bound rather than treating tors_le as a black box.

## B. Ferrero–Washington: the algebraic core and the nonvanishing mechanism

The interface in this project is bounded l-rank of ideal class groups in the cyclotomic layers of a fixed abelian base, not the full classical mu-invariant statement. The proof is an internal construction using a Sinnott-type nonvanishing argument. The following checks its main logical implications; remaining arithmetic providers are marked below.

### B1. Uniform bound on coinvariants is NOT an extension of all invariant characters

[GaloisLibrary/FerreroWashingtonIndex/FerreroWashington.lean:266–333](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/94a362452b3d23ee6172e7b418ad183433030ac6/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/GaloisLibrary/FerreroWashingtonIndex/FerreroWashington.lean#L266-L333) defines E as the finite space of unramified invariant mod-l characters on U=G_K, with K/F cyclic cyclotomic. Its common kernel N is open normal in G_F because E is finite and invariant under conjugation. G_F/N is abelian: U/N is central by the character invariance condition, and the quotient G_F/U is cyclic. [GaloisLibrary/FerreroWashingtonIndex/FerreroWashington.lean:164–205](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/94a362452b3d23ee6172e7b418ad183433030ac6/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/GaloisLibrary/FerreroWashingtonIndex/FerreroWashington.lean#L164-L205) then compares the number of homomorphisms U/N->Z/l with the number from G_F/N->Z/l using the finite abelian subgroup rank inequality. It does NOT claim that every homomorphism of U/N extends to G_F/N, which is false in general (take lZ/l²Z inside Z/l²Z). This cardinality comparison suffices. Inertia outside l is contained in U, so every character on the larger quotient is unramified outside l. finChar provides finiteness at the fixed base F.

Via class field theory pullEq, the dual of the class group coinvariant quotient C/(lC+(1-gamma)C) is the invariant unramified character space. [GaloisLibrary/FerreroWashingtonIndex/FerreroWashington.lean:413–515](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/94a362452b3d23ee6172e7b418ad183433030ac6/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/GaloisLibrary/FerreroWashingtonIndex/FerreroWashington.lean#L413-L515) supplies this comparison and uses the action of an actual generator. [GaloisLibrary/FerreroWashingtonIndex/FerreroWashington.lean:640–706](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/94a362452b3d23ee6172e7b418ad183433030ac6/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/GaloisLibrary/FerreroWashingtonIndex/FerreroWashington.lean#L640-L706) fixes F=Q(mu_(f*l^ee)), ee=1 for odd l and 2 for l=2, and constructs the compatible cyclic generator gamma (or its inverse) at every n>=2; the resulting bound B is chosen before n. The prime-to-l conductor f stays fixed. The class field reciprocity/ramification and finChar providers are still independent audits.

### B2. Why the Sinnott-type lemma is not ordinary linear independence modulo l

[GaloisLibrary/FerreroWashingtonIndex/ToKOf.lean:796–919](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/94a362452b3d23ee6172e7b418ad183433030ac6/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/GaloisLibrary/FerreroWashingtonIndex/ToKOf.lean#L796-L919) works in a finite-support additive group algebra k[A] over an arbitrary commutative ring k. If a has infinite additive order, multiplication by 1-[a] is injective, since an invariant finite-support coefficient function would be constant along the infinite sequence b+na. This proof does not assume k or k[A] is a domain.

For a finite family eta_i whose nonzero integer multiples in different directions do not coincide, let P(t), 0<=t<N, be coefficients. Consider

    E = sum_i (sum_(0<=t<N) P(t)[t eta_i]) prod_(j!=i) (1-[N eta_j]).

If E=0, fix i0 and quotient A by Z N eta_i0. Every summand except i0 dies because its product contains 1-[N eta_i0]. Each other N eta_j has infinite order in the quotient by the assumed integer-multiple independence, so its factor can be cancelled. The remaining elements [t eta_i0], 0<=t<N, are distinct in the quotient. Hence every P(t)=0. This is the actual sinLem proof, including cancellation and coefficient extraction. The independence is pairwise over integer multiples, not full linear independence of all eta_i over Q_l (which would be impossible in the one-dimensional Q_l-vector space).

[GaloisLibrary/FerreroWashingtonIndex/ToKOf.lean:941–1025](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/94a362452b3d23ee6172e7b418ad183433030ac6/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/GaloisLibrary/FerreroWashingtonIndex/ToKOf.lean#L941-L1025) bounds the support of E by a finite set depending only on N and the eta_i, not on the coefficients or k. Distinct elements of that set stay distinct after reduction modulo l^n for all large n, by p-adic separation. Thus a finite-level identity obtained by multiplying the difference equation by the other shift factors lifts to E=0 in the finite-support group algebra of Z_l. No injectivity of reduction on the whole p-adic group is asserted. The finite-level identity is sinFin; its exact coefficients should be audited separately. The eta_i come from reps in EvenTot.lean:73ff, another residual proof of integer-multiple independence.

### B3. The contradiction in nonvan is a coefficient equal to 1

[GaloisLibrary/FerreroWashingtonIndex/ToKOf.lean:1279–1330](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/94a362452b3d23ee6172e7b418ad183433030ac6/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/GaloisLibrary/FerreroWashingtonIndex/ToKOf.lean#L1279-L1330) chooses the threshold n0 using only l,f,c and the fixed representative set, before the field k and character chi. Assuming vanishing of the twisted projection of thetaH at an odd character, twCoef gives the required sum of rescaled functions. charPer makes the extended multiplicative character Psi periodic modulo f*l because the higher kernel is an l-group while k^× has no l-torsion in characteristic l. gcShift and pushDiff yield the difference relation with coefficients

    P(j)=Psi(j) (1 - c * 1_(c divides j)), 0<=j<c*f*l.

The Sinnott-type lemma then forces every P(j)=0. But c>1 and Psi(1)=1, hence P(1)=1, contradiction. This is the specific reason the twisted projection cannot vanish, not an invocation of the target rank theorem. The exact gauss/floor identities gcShift, pushDiff, twCoef and the representative construction require further line-by-line checking; their use and quantifier order are now explicit.

### B4. Nonvanishing gives a UNIFORM nilpotent exponent

[GaloisLibrary/FerreroWashingtonIndex/ToKOf.lean:176–214](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/94a362452b3d23ee6172e7b418ad183433030ac6/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/GaloisLibrary/FerreroWashingtonIndex/ToKOf.lean#L176-L214) takes T=[gamma]-1 in a characteristic-l group algebra, gamma of l-power order. T is nilpotent by the characteristic-l power identity. A twisted element supported in powers of gamma is a polynomial in T. Its nonzero image in the truncated quotient where T^(l^K)=0 shows it is not divisible by T^(l^K). The one-variable nilpotent polynomial lemma phiDvd_nil then gives that the element divides T^(l^K). This remains valid when the truncated quotient has a smaller nilpotence exponent; nonzero image only needs the stated implication.

[GaloisLibrary/FerreroWashingtonIndex/ToKOf.lean:287–364](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/94a362452b3d23ee6172e7b418ad183433030ac6/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/GaloisLibrary/FerreroWashingtonIndex/ToKOf.lean#L287-L364) splits off a finite subgroup D of exponent prime to l via g |-> (g p(g)^-1)^(l^a), where p projects to the cyclic l-direction. The order of D is invertible in k. Over algebraically closed k, the character decomposition (sepChar) lets the proof work on character eigenspaces. The quotient by (theta,1+[J]) enforces J=-1. On a nonzero character eigenspace, Frobenius inversion in k produces a character chi satisfying chi(J)=-1; the twisted polynomial of the previous paragraph divides T^(l^K), and its l^a-th power annihilates that eigenspace by theta=0. Thus T^(l^K*l^a)=0 in the quotient. The proof covers l=2 where -1=1 in k; it does not divide by 2. sepChar and frobEig are residual algebraic providers.

[GaloisLibrary/FerreroWashingtonIndex/ToKOf.lean:413–439](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/94a362452b3d23ee6172e7b418ad183433030ac6/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/GaloisLibrary/FerreroWashingtonIndex/ToKOf.lean#L413-L439) descends the ideal membership from k to Z/l then to Z via a linear retraction of k onto the prime field and coefficientwise lifting; the retraction only needs linearity over the prime field, not multiplicativity. The residual difference lies in l Z[G]. Finally [GaloisLibrary/FerreroWashingtonIndex/ToKOf.lean:1544–1570](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/94a362452b3d23ee6172e7b418ad183433030ac6/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/GaloisLibrary/FerreroWashingtonIndex/ToKOf.lean#L1544-L1570) chooses K=n0 and a=v_l(phi(f*l^ee)), both independent of the level n. The exponent N=l^n0*l^a is therefore uniform. It obtains

    ([gamma]-1)^N in (thetaH, 1+[J], l) in Z[G_n].

The arithmetic decomposition struct, the thetaH convention and the exact twist projection remain to be reviewed, but the uniformity is not obtained by simply using the nilpotence exponent of the entire l-Sylow subgroup (which grows with n).

### B5. From this ideal relation to bounded class group rank

[GaloisLibrary/FerreroWashingtonIndex/FerreroWashington.lean:1364–1405](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/94a362452b3d23ee6172e7b418ad183433030ac6/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/GaloisLibrary/FerreroWashingtonIndex/FerreroWashington.lean#L1364-L1405) applies Stick.coef to the explicit coefficient combination for thetaH(1-J), checking the floor formula using division with remainder. This supplies annihilation of the class group by thetaH(1-J). The actual classical Stickelberger annihilation theorem provider is still outstanding.

[GaloisLibrary/FerreroWashingtonIndex/FerreroWashington.lean:918–1023](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/94a362452b3d23ee6172e7b418ad183433030ac6/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/GaloisLibrary/FerreroWashingtonIndex/FerreroWashington.lean#L918-L1023) lets phi=1-J, psi=1-gamma on the finite abelian class group C (written additively here). Multiply the ideal relation by 1-J; the factor 1+J disappears since J²=1. The theta term annihilates C. Thus phi psi^N C subset l phi C. On the finite group V=phi(C)/l phi(C), psi induces a nilpotent endomorphism T with T^N=0. Its cokernel has size <=[C:lC+psi C]. The equality #V=#phi(C)[l] follows from counting multiplication by l on the finite image group, not from a false equality C[l]=C/lC as subgroups. The nilpotent filtration gives

    #V <= (#coker T)^N <= [C:lC+psi C]^N <= B^N.

Indeed each successive image quotient has size at most #ker T=#coker T (finite group). This is exactly the argument in NilCard.lean:72–135, which also handles N=0. No division of an infinite cardinality occurs.

For odd l, [GaloisLibrary/FerreroWashingtonIndex/FerreroWashington.lean:1235–1260](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/94a362452b3d23ee6172e7b418ad183433030ac6/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/GaloisLibrary/FerreroWashingtonIndex/FerreroWashington.lean#L1235-L1260) uses the minus torsion inclusion in the image of 1-J (2 is invertible on l-torsion), together with the separate reflection inequality to bound the entire l-rank quotient by l times the square of #phi(C)[l]. For l=2, [GaloisLibrary/FerreroWashingtonIndex/FerreroWashington.lean:1268–1325](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/94a362452b3d23ee6172e7b418ad183433030ac6/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/GaloisLibrary/FerreroWashingtonIndex/FerreroWashington.lean#L1268-L1325) instead uses J²=1, D=im(1-J) subset K=ker(1+J), and the index [K:D]. It bounds #C[2] by ([K:D] #D[2])². This follows by counting kernel and image of 1-J on C[2] and mapping K[2] to K/D. The actual signature bound on [K:D] is sgnCyc, not a decomposition into plus/minus eigenspaces in characteristic 2. ReflOdd.lean:762ff and SgnCyc.lean:1293ff are the remaining arithmetic providers.
