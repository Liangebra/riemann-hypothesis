## 1. The previously unresolved local bridge exists

[ExistsLocalEmbeddingOverPlace.lean:136–155](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/05ed7183bb92fecff317e7857d0ce6be9d01f88f/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Proofs/IwasawaDecomposition/ExistsLocalEmbeddingOverPlace.lean#L136-L155) proves exists_isLocEmb for arbitrary number field F, algebraic closure Ω, a chosen valuation ring A over v, and an F-algebra embedding ι:Ω→overline(F_v) such that A is the pullback of the local integral ring. It starts with an arbitrary algebraic-closure embedding, pulls back the integral ring, then conjugates the embedding to recover A.

[DecompositionGroupLocalGaloisIso.lean:70–302](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/05ed7183bb92fecff317e7857d0ce6be9d01f88f/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Proofs/IwasawaDecomposition/DecompositionGroupLocalGaloisIso.lean#L70-L302) proves an actual topological multiplicative equivalence Dec ≃ Gal(F_v), not just equality of group cardinalities. The proof of exists_mulEquiv_galLoc (208–299) is as follows:
1. Approximate coefficients of the minimal polynomial of any local algebraic element by global coefficients; root continuity gives density of ι(Ω) (70–102).
2. The unique spectral norm is preserved by local automorphisms. Restriction to the embedded normal algebraic extension gives a homomorphism from the local absolute Galois group to Dec (225–246).
3. Injectivity follows from density and continuity (247–249).
4. Preservation of the valuation ring by a global decomposition automorphism implies preservation of the norm, because equivalent norms agree at a fixed global element of norm strictly between 0 and 1. Extend the resulting isometry into the completion of the local algebraic closure; continuity makes it fix F_v and algebraicity gives restriction back to the local algebraic closure (104–147, 183–204, 251–276). This yields surjectivity.
5. Continuity of restriction in the Krull topology follows from open stabilizers of finitely many algebraic basis elements (277–294); continuity of the inverse follows from compactness and Hausdorffness (295–299).

The short [exists_algHom_mulEquiv_dec](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/05ed7183bb92fecff317e7857d0ce6be9d01f88f/src/Development/AutomorphicSpectrum/Proofs/BlockDecomposition/ExistsAlgHomMulEquivDec.lean#L55-L62) reverses this isomorphism and retains equivariance. This is the expected standard restriction map. Compare Haran and Jarden et al., [Abelian absolute Galois groups](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/glasgow-mathematical-journal/article/abelian-absolute-galois-groups/0B88295588F7AD513E4354BC9C3FDB30), which uses the henselization and Krasner–Ostrowski comparison.

For the global quotient, [Global.lean:183–210](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/05ed7183bb92fecff317e7857d0ce6be9d01f88f/src/ReciprocityTheory/GaloisValuations/Global.lean#L183-L210) defines NS as the closed normal subgroup generated by all inertia outside S, and locHom as inclusion of Dec followed by the quotient. This is the correct maximal-unramified-outside-S construction. In [PoitouTateNormalisedHolds.lean:110–154](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/05ed7183bb92fecff317e7857d0ce6be9d01f88f/src/Development/WeilBound/CoreStatements/PublishedTheorems/PoitouTateDuality/PoitouTateNineTerm/PoitouTateNormalisedHolds.lean#L110-L154), localCmp2' actually calls exists_algHom_mulEquiv_dec and proves commutativity with the separate ground-theory gsFinFamily via the global quotient comparison. foundData_nonempty (176–193) stores the global and local equivalences, continuity, coefficient and dual comparisons. Thus the bridge is used by the provider, not merely present elsewhere. The full inertia comparison for all local statements remains a separate review obligation; the specific Frobenius implication used by normalization is found below.

## 2. Continuous cohomology and cup conventions

[SeparatedDvrSecondCohomologyMem.lean:68–174](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/05ed7183bb92fecff317e7857d0ce6be9d01f88f/src/ReciprocityTheory/GaloisValuations/SeparatedDvrSecondCohomologyMem.lean#L68-L174) defines continuous cochains, H0 as invariants, Z1 by f(gh)=f(g)+g f(h), d0 by g m−m, Z2 by g f(h,k)−f(gh,k)+f(g,hk)−f(g,h)=0, and d1 by g c(h)−c(gh)+c(g). H1 and H2 are the quotients by the corresponding coboundaries; in degree two the primitive c must be continuous. These are the standard inhomogeneous continuous cohomology definitions, not an abstract replacement. The general dn uses Fin.contractNth: for the final index it drops the final argument, giving the usual last face with sign (−1)^(n+1). Compare [Mathlib Fin.contractNth](https://leanprover-community.github.io/mathlib4_docs/Mathlib/Data/Fin/Tuple/Basic.html#Fin.contractNth). A fully checked all-degree comparison remains separate.

The same file (180–220) implements restriction by precomposition in each argument and proves it respects coboundaries. [ContinuousConstSMulQuotient.lean:76–110](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/05ed7183bb92fecff317e7857d0ce6be9d01f88f/src/ReciprocityTheory/GaloisValuations/ContinuousConstSMulQuotient.lean#L76-L110) implements the inhomogeneous degree-one cup by b(x(g),g y(h)), with the required coefficient equivariance. For a trivial-action character in the second slot this is exactly κ(g)χ(h). The differential and cup signs used in our independent normalization calculation are therefore the ones present in the project.

The source [FinalInv.lean:500–545](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/05ed7183bb92fecff317e7857d0ce6be9d01f88f/src/Development/WeilBound/CoreStatements/PublishedTheorems/PoitouTateDuality/FinalInv.lean#L500-L545) independently contains the same explicit cochain argument as our prior note: bf(h)=Y^urChar(h). [ExistsInvFamNormalised.lean:1415–1452](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/05ed7183bb92fecff317e7857d0ce6be9d01f88f/src/Development/AutomorphicSpectrum/Proofs/BlockDecomposition/ExistsInvFamNormalised.lean#L1415-L1452) constructs the normalized bijection: take an arbitrary linear equivalence H2→Z/n and, if necessary, multiply it by the inverse of the image of a generator. cup_class_eq ensures all prescribed generator representatives give the same class. exists_invFam_normalised (1470–1486) makes a single family by choice independently of S.

Crucially, [FinalInv.lean:1280–1320](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/05ed7183bb92fecff317e7857d0ce6be9d01f88f/src/Development/WeilBound/CoreStatements/PublishedTheorems/PoitouTateDuality/FinalInv.lean#L1280-L1320) proves the comparison for **every** matched cocycle pair, not only a generator: zmodToQZ(inv A [z]) = −gsLocalInvRootsQZ([x]). ## 4. The three Poitou–Tate arrows do not all have the same relative sign

Distinguish the effect of negating all local invariants (each project tr_i changes sign) from the comparison with classical *primal-first* cup products (signs −,+,−). The middle arrow uses the dual-first cup and acquires a second negative sign by degree-one graded commutativity.

[NineAddCore.lean](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/05ed7183bb92fecff317e7857d0ce6be9d01f88f/src/Development/WeilBound/CoreStatements/PublishedTheorems/PoitouTateDuality/PoitouTateNineTerm/NineAddCore.lean) proves:
- locTr0_B (126–197): negative relative sign.
- locTr1_C (341–415): positive relative sign; dual-first transfer; this is not an assumption that cup products commute in degree one.
- locTr2' (511–576): negative relative sign.
- nineAdd_of_found3 (903–918): passes exactly the sign pattern −,+,− into the nine-term transport.
- nineAdd_core (1155–1216): uses poitouTate_nineTerm_gs, the six global/local cohomology comparisons and all three signed comparison lemmas.

Multiplying individual linear arrows by −1 does not change their images or kernels; exactness is preserved. Multiplying individual *local summands* by arbitrary signs would not justify this. The pattern is constant across all places for each degree. The source already handles the relevant signs, so an additional production lemma is not required merely to repair this interface.
