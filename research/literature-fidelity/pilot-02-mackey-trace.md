## 1. Main finding: a unit-scaling argument replaces exact normalized trace compatibility

[RestrictedRamification/BaseLevel.lean:60–103,132–175](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/9cd03154363eeee40e9173fa2c0cf907f5790093/src/GroundTheory/ProfiniteCohomology/RestrictedRamification/BaseLevel.lean#L60-L103) uses a weaker condition than the classical normalized identity Tr_F cor = Tr_L (under the indicated arithmetic base-change isomorphism). It proves that the two maps differ by multiplication by a unit of Z/n. This is sufficient for the asserted perfect pairing. It is not evidence that the classical exact trace identity is false or absent everywhere in the repository.

Indeed let W=H_c^3(G_{L,S_L},mu_n), T_L:W -> Z/n be bijective, and f=T_F cor e:W -> Z/n surjective, where e transports W to the Mackey family over the open subgroup. Choose w_1 with T_L(w_1)=1. For every w, w=T_L(w).val*w_1 by injectivity. Hence f(w)=c*T_L(w), c=f(w_1). By surjectivity there is w with f(w)=1, so c is a unit, including when n is a composite prime power. Multiplication by c preserves perfectness. The source proves both injectivity and surjectivity of the scaled pairing, not just equal cardinalities. It does not require the extension degree to be invertible modulo n. In particular this argument is valid when l divides [L:F].

The actual application [BijectiveGsLocalInvRootsOfPlaces.lean:1283–1320](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/9cd03154363eeee40e9173fa2c0cf907f5790093/src/GroundTheory/ProfiniteCohomology/RestrictedRamification/BijectiveGsLocalInvRootsOfPlaces.lean#L1283-L1320) proves all needed conditions: places above l remain in S_L, S_L is finite and nonempty, L contains mu_n if its open subgroup fixes mu_n, and the odd/totally imaginary condition persists. It obtains the level pairing, the surjectivity of the base trace, and the surjectivity of compact corestriction, then calls the unit-scaling lemma. [PoitouTateNineTermGs.lean:1383–1435](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/9cd03154363eeee40e9173fa2c0cf907f5790093/src/GroundTheory/ProfiniteCohomology/PoitouTate/PoitouTateNineTermGs.lean#L1383-L1435) actually supplies these conditions to the general duality theorem. The run_tac blocks in these inspected bodies construct terms from an already proven local hypothesis via mkExpectedPropHint, rather than visibly inserting an axiom; their actual kernel behavior is not tested by this note.

Classification: proof-method distinction, no demonstrated mathematical defect. Keep narrowMatch for GC-009; do not infer a failure of the final fixed-field normalized trace or nine-term maps from this intermediate proof's use of a unit. The exact extension-natural trace identity is not established by the inspected unit-scaling lemma.

## 2. Why compact corestriction is surjective without dividing by the index

[ShapiroDefs.lean:94–142](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/9cd03154363eeee40e9173fa2c0cf907f5790093/src/GroundTheory/ProfiniteCohomology/ShapiroDefs.lean#L94-L142) defines the coinduced trace using the coset sum sum_(xU) x f(x^-1), proves independence of representatives and equivariance. Its composite with the coinduction unit is multiplication by [G:U], but this is NOT the argument used for surjectivity.

[BaseCores.lean:71–130](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/9cd03154363eeee40e9173fa2c0cf907f5790093/src/GroundTheory/ProfiniteCohomology/RestrictedRamification/BaseCores.lean#L71-L130) constructs a preimage of a in A: f_a(g)=g a when g lies in U and 0 otherwise. This satisfies the coinduction equivariance relation; only the identity coset contributes to the trace and gives a. Thus the trace is surjective on coefficients, even if the index kills A. The construction is a set-level section, not claimed to be a G-equivariant splitting. The short exact sequence 0 -> K -> Coind_U^G Res A -> A -> 0 has finite, discrete, n-torsion kernel whenever A is finite discrete n-torsion. By the compact-support long exact sequence,

    H_c^3(G,Coind Res A) -> H_c^3(G,A) -> H_c^4(G,K)=0.

So the first map is surjective. Compact Mackey-Shapiro identifies its domain with the compact cohomology over the open subgroup with the corresponding local family. [BaseCores.lean:146–188](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/9cd03154363eeee40e9173fa2c0cf907f5790093/src/GroundTheory/ProfiniteCohomology/RestrictedRamification/BaseCores.lean#L146-L188) proves exactly this and invokes the independently established H_c^4 vanishing reviewed last round. [PoincarePairs/IsDiscreteRepResHomCoindRes.lean:127–135](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/9cd03154363eeee40e9173fa2c0cf907f5790093/src/GroundTheory/ProfiniteCohomology/PoincarePairs/IsDiscreteRepResHomCoindRes.lean#L127-L135) defines compactCores as inverse Mackey-Shapiro followed by the coinduced trace. No circular appeal to compact duality in this argument.

## 3. Arithmetic Mackey indices really are places over S

[BaseChangeMackey.lean:327–379](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/9cd03154363eeee40e9173fa2c0cf907f5790093/src/GroundTheory/ProfiniteCohomology/RestrictedRamification/BaseChangeMackey.lean#L327-L379) identifies the disjoint union of double cosets U\G/im(G_v) with the places of L above S. The code uses the place defined by the local embedding composed with the inverse of the coset representative. It proves both directions: equal places imply embeddings differ by a local automorphism and hence the same double coset; any place admits a suitable embedding and thus a representative. This does not assume local maps into G_S injective, or that L/F is Galois.

Lines [417–467](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/9cd03154363eeee40e9173fa2c0cf907f5790093/src/GroundTheory/ProfiniteCohomology/RestrictedRamification/BaseChangeMackey.lean#L417-L467) identify the preimage subgroup phi_v^-1(x^-1 U x) in the local absolute Galois group with Gal(overline(L_w)/L_w), using completion embeddings and a topological group equivalence. The conjugation map to U commutes with the actual arithmetic localization after global base change. Distinguishing this preimage from an intersection inside G_S matters when localization has a kernel. [BaseTransportGS.lean:444–483](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/9cd03154363eeee40e9173fa2c0cf907f5790093/src/GroundTheory/ProfiniteCohomology/RestrictedRamification/BaseTransportGS.lean#L444-L483) transports the full compact cohomology and the cup pairing through these equivalences, not merely their cardinalities.

## 4. Mackey-Shapiro is constructed on the cone with a homotopy correction

[Shapiro.lean:257–324](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/9cd03154363eeee40e9173fa2c0cf907f5790093/src/GroundTheory/ProfiniteCohomology/Shapiro.lean#L257-L324) proves the Shapiro evaluation map is an isomorphism by degree induction on all discrete representations. Degree zero uses evaluation at 1 on invariants. In higher degrees use the exact coinduced resolution, acyclicity of discrete coinduced modules, naturality of the connecting morphisms, and separate surjectivity and injectivity arguments. The acyclicity provider remains an independent dependency.

[MackeyFormula/CompactSupport.lean:394–470](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/9cd03154363eeee40e9173fa2c0cf907f5790093/src/GroundTheory/ProfiniteCohomology/MackeyFormula/CompactSupport.lean#L394-L470) constructs a global Shapiro map alpha and local Mackey map beta; the localization square commutes up to an explicit homotopy h, not necessarily on the nose. In the convention d(x,y)=(dx,f(x)-dy), write beta f-alpha followed by f' = d'h+hd, i.e. beta f-f'alpha=d'h+hd. [MackeyFormula/ConeHomotopy.lean:63–114](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/9cd03154363eeee40e9173fa2c0cf907f5790093/src/GroundTheory/ProfiniteCohomology/MackeyFormula/ConeHomotopy.lean#L63-L114) defines the cone comparison (x,y) -> (alpha x,beta y-h x). Direct substitution shows this is a chain map. Without the -h x term the map would generally not commute with the differential. The source establishes the global and local quasi-isomorphisms and uses the cone long exact sequence to get a compact cohomology isomorphism. This is genuine compact Shapiro, not an unjustified application of ordinary Shapiro to compact-support groups.

The coinduced pointwise cup pairing and ordinary Shapiro are compatible by evaluation at identity. Compatibility for the cone is more subtle because of the homotopy correction. [PoincarePairs/MackeyCup.lean:676–705](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/9cd03154363eeee40e9173fa2c0cf907f5790093/src/GroundTheory/ProfiniteCohomology/PoincarePairs/MackeyCup.lean#L676-L705) invokes explicit identities for the homotopy of the cup product, including separately degree-zero left/right cases, and the cone pairing lemma. [PoincarePairs.lean:197–274](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/9cd03154363eeee40e9173fa2c0cf907f5790093/src/GroundTheory/ProfiniteCohomology/PoincarePairs.lean#L197-L274) uses this equality and the coefficient duality comparison to transfer the perfect pairing for the trivial cyclic module over U to its coinduction over G. The underlying prism homotopy identity and cone product proof merit separate line-by-line verification.

## 5. Trace bijectivity is independently supplied

[BaseInv.lean:286–349](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/9cd03154363eeee40e9173fa2c0cf907f5790093/src/GroundTheory/ProfiniteCohomology/RestrictedRamification/BaseInv.lean#L286-L349) proves injectivity of the trace on H_c^3(mu_n): take a local degree-two preimage (using global H^3=0); its invariant sum vanishes, so use the S-unit Brauer comparison to produce a primary S-unit class with those local components. Its n-multiple maps to zero in the Brauer group, so primary injection kills the n-multiple. Kummer lifts it to H^2(G_S,mu_n). Local invariant injectivity identifies every restriction with the given tuple, which maps to zero in H_c^3 by cone exactness. Surjectivity uses a single local invariant and a nonempty S. Thus the bijection is not being deduced from the same general compact duality theorem that needs it.

## 6. Proposed focused follow-up (not implemented)

If exact normalized naturality is desired, a clean sufficient lemma is the local-to-compact/corestriction square: compact corestriction composed with the transported local-to-compact map equals local-to-compact composed with the sum, over w above v, of local corestrictions. Prove it from the actual inverse compact Mackey map and coset trace, accounting for the cone inclusion and chosen embeddings. Then use the local invariant identity inv_v cor_(w/v)=inv_w (not the restriction formula involving the degree), sum over all v, and surjectivity of local-to-compact in degree two. The desired exact trace identity follows on all H_c^3 classes, so c=1 in the preceding comparison. This is a candidate extension of the audit, not a claim that the lemma is missing from every module or has been compiled.
