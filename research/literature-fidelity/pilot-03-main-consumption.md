## 2. Direct consumer: the odd-prime reciprocity argument

This is the strongest located evidence of actual use in the VWin branch, because it selects the specific exactness implication rather than merely passing a whole PT theorem.

[PrimitiveFrobeniusFixed/Iv.lean:115–145](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/ba791d9dda19edf070f1863818877ed76d23600d/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/PrimitiveFrobeniusFixed/Iv.lean#L115-L145), private theorem glob, takes an odd prime l, positive r, finite S containing primes over l, chosen valuations over S, and a continuous degree-two cocycle w with cyclotomic coefficient action. It assumes w is unchanged under right multiplication in each variable by the restricted-ramification normal subgroup NS. It constructs the descended cocycle W on G_S by exists_descend_pair_quotient, checks the ordinary cocycle identity with the descended cyclotomic character mu, and invokes the .1.sum_eq_zero field of NineTermExact at the rank-one coefficient charRep mu. This is precisely the last local invariant-sum arrow because charRep mu has trivial Tate dual; see PoitouTateNineTermTransgressionZeroApply for the lemma. The statement is not that an arbitrary local class must vanish.

If w is a local coboundary at every place except v0, the source proves that condition equivalent to restriction of [W] being zero in H² (using separated_dvr_second_cohomology_mem). All other summands vanish, so the global reciprocity equality forces the local invariant at v0 to vanish. Since invAt is injective, the restriction there is zero too. This is a correct conventional reciprocity deduction from the nine-term sequence and invariant bijectivity; the actual proof uses exactly those two facts.

[Iv.lean:318–386](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/ba791d9dda19edf070f1863818877ed76d23600d/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/PrimitiveFrobeniusFixed/Iv.lean#L318-L386), private theorem core, instantiates w(g,h)=lambda(n(g)*alpha(g)*c(h)), where c and n are one-cocycles for characters alpha and beta with alpha beta cyclotomic after scalar extension. lambda is additive and compatible with Z/l^r scalars. It explicitly checks the cup cocycle identity, continuity and descent; chooses S as the finite set of primes dividing Nq, proves l|N implies all primes above l belong to S, q is a different prime, and uses actual valuations above each place. At the other primes c is a coboundary, giving an explicit primitive -lambda(n(g)*alpha(g)*m). At q, the newly obtained coboundary condition combines with local unramified orthogonality to show lambda(n(tau)*c(g))=0 for tau in inertia and g in decomposition. The conditions l odd and r positive are not silently dropped. This is a genuine Selmer-style reciprocity constraint on two cocycles. Lines [391–399,1510–1513](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/ba791d9dda19edf070f1863818877ed76d23600d/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/PrimitiveFrobeniusFixed/Iv.lean#L1510-L1513) assemble recip and iv, used by HVCore.of_four_alt. Detailed proofs of ns_le and locq, the finite coefficient quotient and PsiBound conversion remain to review.

[Formula.lean:57–95](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/ba791d9dda19edf070f1863818877ed76d23600d/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/GaloisLibrary/GaloisCohomologyLength/Formula.lean#L57-L95) invokes finite_level for all positive r, derives ptCokerAt and ptShaAt and local Tate duality at the limit. It proves the length identity for the global-to-local degree-two map f by the two short exact sequences for kernel, image and cokernel:

    length(X)+length(coker f)=length(ker f)+length(Y).

In its notation this yields

    VLen(D,e)+nuGal(l,F,|e|)=shaLen(D,e)+sumLocalH0TwistLength(D,e).

There is no cancellation of infinity. [LemmaKOfLitN.lean:1098–1155](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/ba791d9dda19edf070f1863818877ed76d23600d/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/ModularityArgument/ThresholdArguments/DenseThresholdCases/LemmaKOfLitN.lean#L1098-L1155), sFinal, uses this inequality over a totally complex field L (PTCase via ptCase_of_isTotallyComplex), a ShaK bound s, and a local length estimate n(2+log_l n+e) with n=[L:Q]. ### Limit passage is a high-priority interface, not a trivial consequence of finite duality

[LengthEqLengthInvariantsOfTorsionLevels.lean:1278–1282](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/ba791d9dda19edf070f1863818877ed76d23600d/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Proofs/FoundationProofs/LengthEqLengthInvariantsOfTorsionLevels.lean#L1278-L1282) derives the cokernel length using actual torsion embeddings and card_cok. At [1347–1400](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/ba791d9dda19edf070f1863818877ed76d23600d/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Proofs/FoundationProofs/LengthEqLengthInvariantsOfTorsionLevels.lean#L1347-L1400), ptShaAt requires eventual saturation of the localization image and eventual ability to choose a bounded-torsion coboundary representative. It splits e=0 and e≠0. In the trivial twist, the finite-level cokernel count proves torsion-freeness of the integral cokernel, giving saturation; the coboundary condition is trivial. For e≠0, local cyclotomic powers have a nontrivial action, so local invariants in Q_l/Z_l are killed by a fixed l-power; the local H² product has finite length and a fixed annihilating power via Artinian stabilization plus l-adic separation. These bounds supply both eventual conditions. Only then does the source apply the cardinality-to-length lemma using a descending separated filtration on the integral Sha kernel and an ascending exhaustion on torsion Sha¹. This is not an illicit inference that arbitrary finite-level isomorphisms are compatible as inverse systems. The precise hypotheses/proofs of global_lim, cok_ker, lpow_sep, sha1_exhausted_by_torsion_levels and padicInt_length_eq_of_card_quotient_eq_card_submodule still require independent scrutiny. This is now the most important residual mathematical interface in the length branch.

## 4. Greenberg–Wiles: a separate actual arithmetic consumer, not yet connected field-by-field to Main

[GreenbergWiles.lean:805–866](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/ba791d9dda19edf070f1863818877ed76d23600d/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/ThetaCorrespondence/GaloisRepresentations/GreenbergWiles.lean#L805-L866) extracts the nine-term sequence and Sha numerical duality from the normalized PT provider. It uses them to derive global finiteness, identify the image of localization with the annihilator of the dual localization image, count the preimages of local conditions using perfect local pairings, cancel Sha cardinalities, and use the global and local Euler characteristics. This is a substantive use of the exactness and duality, not just citation.

[GreenbergWilesDualSelmerLowerBoundModPrimePow.lean:1800–1875](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/ba791d9dda19edf070f1863818877ed76d23600d/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Proofs/FoundationProofs/GreenbergWilesDualSelmerLowerBoundModPrimePow.lean#L1800-L1875) calls hL.gw at Q with positive n, odd l from Nf.l_odd, primes above l included by Nf.above, continuous reduction of an integral rank-two representation, actual lifted cyclotomic character and complex conjugation representatives. It obtains the global Selmer cardinality equality. Away from the selected l-adic place the unramified local condition has cardinality H⁰; at that selected place it is bounded by CA times the coefficient size. After cancelling positive common factors and bounding global H⁰, the code proves

    #Sel_G(n) <= C0 CA #Sel_D(n),

with C0,CA independent of n. It explicitly injects pairwise noncohomologous cocycles into the primal Selmer group and chooses dual cocycle representatives to obtain a family at least 1/C as large. [LocPublishedOfLitCoh.lean:1174–1218,1274–1280,1890–1897](https://github.com/Liangebra/riemann-hypothesis/blob/ba791d9dda19edf070f1863818877ed76d23600d/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Proofs/FoundationProofs/LocPublishedOfLitCoh.lean#L1274-L1280) uses gw_ineq to force dual classes of unbounded order from a nonzero ordinary Selmer class; dual vanishing then implies primal vanishing, used by es_vanish to deduce BlochKatoSelmerVanishes under Nf and ES. The proof of ES, the uniform bounds, local condition transport, and the caller of es_vanish into the top nonendoscopic theorem are residual checks. In particular a local actual use of LitCoh.gw does not by itself establish final Main dependence.
